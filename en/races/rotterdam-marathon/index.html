NN Marathon Rotterdam

RunningApril 11, 2027Rotterdam, Netherlands
Distance
42.195 km
Fee
About €155 (2026 reference: €5 pre-registration plus about €150); 2027 fee to be confirmed
Entry
Free pre-registration 20 October to 3 November 2026 (10:00); registration opens 4 November 2026 at 14:00, first come first served, no lottery
Details
Start at the foot of the Erasmus Bridge, finish on the Coolsingel
Time limit
6 hours

Getting there

Rotterdam The Hague airport (RTM), about 20 minutes from the centre; or Amsterdam Schiphol (AMS), 35–40 minutes by train.

Good to know

Flat course, among Europe's fastest in spring.
