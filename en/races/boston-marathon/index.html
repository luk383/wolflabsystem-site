One of the Abbott World Marathon Majors; run on Patriots' Day Monday. Age-group standards, for example 2:55 for men and 3:25 for women aged 18–34
Time limit
See official rules

Getting there

Logan airport (BOS); the start is in Hopkinton, with organised shuttles from the city.

Good to know

A qualifying time doesn't guarantee entry: what counts is how far under the standard you are. Charity entries are the alternative.
