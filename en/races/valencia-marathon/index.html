Races

Maratón Valencia

RunningDecember 6, 2026Valencia, Spain
Distance
42.195 km
Fee
€80 (first 10,000 bibs), then €180; waiting list €200
Entry
Allocated by priority and lottery in December 2025; for 2027 it reopens in December
Details
Finish at the City of Arts and Sciences
Time limit
5 hours 30 minutes
Course
Flat course (official description). Elevation gain in metres not given on the official page consulted. Official source

Getting there

Valencia airport (VLC), linked to the centre by metro.

Good to know

Flat, sea-level course with average temperatures of 9–15 °C: one of Europe's fastest marathons. Many travel packages include bib and hotel.
