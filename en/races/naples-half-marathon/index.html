Napoli City Half Marathon

RunningFebruary 21, 2027Naples, Italy
Distance
21.097 km
Fee
€38 (first 2,000), €45 (up to 4,000), €52 until closing; €60 at the Expo
Entry
Entries open in tiers
Details
Start in Fuorigrotta (Viale Kennedy, Mostra d'Oltremare), finish in Piazza Tecchio, 9:00 am
Time limit
3 hours 30 minutes (cut-off at km 9.5 by 10:40 am)

Getting there

Naples Capodichino airport (NAP); Napoli Centrale station for high-speed trains. Fuorigrotta is served by metro and the Cumana line.

Good to know

About 17 km along the seafront with Vesuvius in the background. There is also a two-person relay (9.5 + 11.6 km).

Details checked on October 26, 2026 against the sources listed. Fees and dates can change: always check the official site before entering or booking. Source: finishers.com
