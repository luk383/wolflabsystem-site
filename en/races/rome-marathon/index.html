Run Rome The Marathon

RunningMarch 14, 2027Rome, Italy
Distance
42.195 km
Fee
€129 from 16/09/2026 until close (€99 until 31/05, €109 until 31/07, €119 until 15/09; online service fee excluded)
Entry
Entries open until 7 March 2027 (unless sold out); in 2026 it sold out four months early
Details
Start at about 8:30 am
Time limit
7 hours

Getting there

Fiumicino (FCO) and Ciampino (CIA) airports; Roma Termini station for high-speed trains.

Good to know

The fee includes a technical shirt, medal and bag drop. The centre is closed to traffic: use the metro.
