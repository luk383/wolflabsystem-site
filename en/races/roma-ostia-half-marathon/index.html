RomaOstia Half Marathon

RunningFebruary 28, 2027Rome, Italy
Distance
21.097 km
Fee
See official site
Entry
52nd edition
Details
Start in the EUR district, finish on the Ostia seafront along Via Cristoforo Colombo
Time limit
3 hours 30 minutes
Course
Flat EUR streets; the Camping climb starts around km 10: first part about 1.5 km with a maximum gradient of three degrees, then another 1,400 m that is gentler (organiser's description). Official source

Getting there

Fiumicino (FCO) and Ciampino (CIA) airports; EUR is on metro line B, Ostia on the Roma–Lido train.

Good to know

Fast course with a single climb, the 'salita del Camping', and a downhill finish towards the sea.
