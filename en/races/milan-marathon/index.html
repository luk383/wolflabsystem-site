Distance
42.195 km

Entries open until 31 March 2027

Start at 8:15 am

6 hours 30 minutes (cut-offs at km 21.1 and km 27.8)

Elevation gain of about 100 m and 3.3 km of cobbles, according to the official 2025 handbook (may change). Official source
