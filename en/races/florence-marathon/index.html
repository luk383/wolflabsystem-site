Getting there

Florence Peretola airport (about 20 minutes from the centre); alternatively Pisa (about 1 hour) or Bologna (about 40 minutes by high-speed train). Santa Maria Novella station is a few minutes from the Expo.

Good to know

On race morning the centre is closed to cars: stay within walking distance or near tram line T1.
