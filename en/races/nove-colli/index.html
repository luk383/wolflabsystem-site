CyclingMay 23, 2027Cesenatico, Italy
Distance
2 road routes + gravel
Fee

Entry

Details
Nove Colli Village in Cesenatico
Time limit
See official rules
Course
Elevation stated by the organiser: long course 200 km with 3,814 m (maximum gradient 18%), medium course 137 km with 1,872 m. The gravel course is not covered by these figures. Official source

Getting there

Bologna (BLQ) and Rimini (RMI) airports; Cesenatico station on the Ravenna–Rimini line.

The first tier is cheapest: enter in December. Riviera hotels often have cyclist packages.
