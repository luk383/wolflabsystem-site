Running shoes: Daily, Tempo, Marathon.

RTINGS lab measurements and use scores, Chinese brands included. No tests of our own and no ranking.

  • How we split the categories: RTINGS does not use the word "Tempo". For Daily and Marathon we use its use scores ("Daily Training", "Half/Full Marathon Racing"). For Tempo we group shoes that RTINGS reviews describe for fast workouts and short races. The grouping is ours; the numbers are RTINGS'.
  • The 0-10 scores and measurements are RTINGS' on one pair (men's size) and change as their test bench updates. Prices are left out: they vary by market.

Daily

Everyday miles. By use score the ASICS Novablast 6 is highest; Evo SL and Ghost 18 score the same. No Chinese-brand daily shoe read so far.

Tempo

Fast workouts and short races. RTINGS describes the ANTA C202 7 as more practical "for workouts and general faster training sessions" than the race version; the XTEP 260X 3.0 as a "short-distance workout and racing shoe"; the Endorphin Speed 6 as a meaningful upgrade "especially for longer workouts and races".

Marathon

Carbon-plated race shoes. Highest RTINGS half and full marathon scores: Li-Ning Feidian 6 Elite (8.4), then XTEP 160X 7.0 PRO (8.2) and Adizero Adios Pro 4 (8.1). Outside (Running) calls the Feidian 6 Elite and XTEP 160X 7.0 PRO Chinese carbon-plated "marathon racers".
