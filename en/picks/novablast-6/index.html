Good for

  • Runners who want one shoe for all their weekly mileage
  • Midfoot or forefoot strikers who want a cushioned, lively ride
  • Runners who put impact protection first

Less suited to

  • Heel strikers who want a lively shoe (the heel returns less)
  • Runners who want snap for intervals or short races (weight and soft forefoot, RTINGS)
