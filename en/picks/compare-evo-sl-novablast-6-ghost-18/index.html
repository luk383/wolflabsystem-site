Which to choose? (reasoned from the data, not a verdict)

  • Mixed paces, the odd race: Adizero Evo SL. The lightest of the three (220 g) with the highest energy return.
  • Maximum protection and everyday comfort: Novablast 6. Taller stack and very high cushioning; the forefoot is livelier than the heel.
  • Stability and easy miles, heel landing: Ghost 18. The heaviest and least responsive, but the most stable according to RTINGS.
