WOLF PICKS · RUNNING · TRAINING SHOES

adidas Adizero Evo SL

Lightstrike Pro midsole with no carbon plate, light. In RTINGS tests, cushioning and energy return are high for the weight.

Measured data

Weight220 g
Heel stack36.9 mm
Drop6.7 mm
Energy return, forefoot83.3%
Energy return, heel81.4%
List price€150 (adidas.it, checked 28/10/2026)

RTINGS lab measurements on one pair (men's size): manufacturer figures can differ. Checked on 28/10/2026. List price: price shown by the brand on its own site (Italian store), men's version, checked on 28/10/2026. These are not retailer offers and can change by variant and market.

Good for

  • Runners who want a shoe for daily miles, faster sessions and the odd race
  • Runners who want cushioning without a tall stack

Less suited to

  • Runners who need support or roll inwards (RTINGS)
  • Runners who want maximum stability when tired: the platform is narrow at the heel and hollow under the arch
  • Wet grip rated poor by RTINGS
