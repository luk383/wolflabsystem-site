WOLF PICKS · CYCLING · BIKE COMPUTERS

GPS bike computers: how to choose.

What to look at, and three models with declared specs. No ranking.

What to look at

  • Navigation: loaded route, turn-by-turn prompts, maps on the device or on the phone.
  • Battery: each brand states hours under different conditions. Check the condition (screen, navigation, sensors) and leave margin for your longest ride.
  • Screen: size, sunlight readability, use in rain or with gloves.
  • Compatibility: sensors, electronic shifting, e-bikes, the apps you already use.
  • Memory and maps: room for maps and smoothness.
