Road to Berlin 2027

A marathon prepared as an amateur, with shift-work hours, part of the aerobic training on the bike and every choice explained with sources.

— days to the start

Sunday 26 September 2027, Berlin. The course is marked by a blue line painted on the road: it's the one you see across this site.

The phases

  1. Oct 2026 – Feb 2027Base. Lots of easy volume across running and cycling, two strength sessions a week, a gradual build in mileage.
  2. Mar – May 2027Build. Growing long run, first marathon-pace and threshold work. Cycling shifts to recovery rides.
  3. Jun – mid Sep 2027Specific. The 12–16 weeks that count: race-pace sessions, specific long runs, fuelling rehearsals.
  4. Last 2–3 weeksTaper. Less volume, intensity maintained, carb loading in the final two days.
