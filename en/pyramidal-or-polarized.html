DECISION GUIDE

Pyramidal or polarized?
What you should do.

The 2025 meta-analysis finds no winner. No rule by weekly hours is supported by the evidence. Here is what you can decide.

What we know

Both models keep most work easy, below the first threshold. They differ in the split between medium and high zones. For VO₂max and time trials the 2025 meta-analysis finds no overall superiority, with possible differences by athlete level. Evidence: moderate.

What to do, in order

  1. Measure your current work. For two weeks count time in three zones, always with the same method (thresholds, not just your watch zones).
  2. If the middle zone dominates and it is not intended, move that work: either easier, or into a few real quality sessions. Do not add hard sessions just to hit a percentage.
  3. Pick what you can repeat. With little time, or strength and running in the same week, hard work costs more recovery: keep few hard days (Wolf Engine proposes 1–2) and the rest easy.
  4. Review after a block. Change one thing at a time, for 6–8 weeks, and compare with the same test.

Points 3 and 4 are Wolf Lab rules of thumb, not study results.
