METHODOLOGY

How we evaluate.

Evidence before opinion. Every number has a source and every estimate says what it does not know.

Evidence hierarchy

  1. L1Systematic review / meta-analysis
  2. L2Randomized controlled trial
  3. L3Controlled study
  4. L4Observational study
  5. L5Expert opinion
  6. L6Manufacturer claim

A simplification: a good observational study can beat a small, poorly run trial. Level is the starting point; then we look at quality, population and consistency.

Wolf Score

The Wolf Score is a 0 to 100 score with public criteria and weights. It appears only if every criterion has a source or our own test. Products need a test actually run. Today no Wolf Score is published.

How it would be computed (experimental proposal v0.1, not validated)

  1. Each criterion gets a 0 to 100 value from a rule written in advance, tied to a sourced data point or to our own test with a protocol.
  2. Score = average of the criteria, with equal weights. Equal weights are a provisional, arbitrary choice, not a scientific result.
  3. If a criterion is missing, the score is not computed. No estimates for missing data.
  4. The sources, check date and Wolf Confidence always appear next to the score.

Publishing formula and weights lets anyone repeat the calculation, but does not make it valid. Validity (whether the score really measures what matters) has not been shown: until data and tests exist we publish no score.

NEWS → ANALYSIS → TEST → VERDICT

News: what came out, with manufacturer claims. Analysis: what available data say. Test: we tried it, with a protocol. Verdict: would I buy it, only after a test. Today we publish News only.

My Wolf

My Wolf saves the Wolf Engine values only on this device, in the browser, and only if you press Save. There is no account and nothing is sent to Wolf Lab. You can delete everything with one button.

Wolf Engine

Wolf Engine uses published formulas (Daniels and Gilbert VDOT, Coggan zones, Friel heart-rate zones, ACSM guidelines). Calculation runs inside your browser. The hybrid-week rules are ours, declared as rules of thumb, not study results.

Use of artificial intelligence

Text and research are prepared with AI assistance and then checked against sources. No test is presented as real unless it is.
