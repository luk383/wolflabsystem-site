Trips

Calpe and the Costa Blanca

BikeAlicante, Spain
When
December to March
Altitude
Sea level; hills and passes inland

Why go

Mild winter weather, quiet inland roads and the highest concentration of pro teams on camp in December and January.

What to train

Winter aerobic base: long rides, steady climbs such as Coll de Rates, threshold work on 15–25 minute climbs.

Costs

Coastal apartment prices are low in winter; bike hire available in Calpe, Altea and Benidorm.

Getting there

Alicante airport (ALC), about an hour's drive from Calpe; alternatively Valencia.

Good to know

Days are short in December: start early. The coast is also handy for easy running along the seafront.
