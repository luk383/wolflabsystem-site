Trips

Iten, Kenya

Altitude · runningRift Valley, Kenya
When
All year; average temperatures between 18 and 24 °C
Altitude
About 2,400 m

Why go

The "home of champions": running the red dirt roads alongside hundreds of Kenyan athletes, with organised camps including food, lodging and often a coach.

What to train

Lots of easy volume on dirt roads, group fartlek, track sessions at Kamariny Stadium.

Costs

A week's camp costs about $210–1,200 depending on services; a centre with lodging and four meals a day starts at about $50 per day. Flights extra.

Getting there

Fly to Nairobi, then a domestic flight or transfer to Eldoret, about an hour from Iten.

Good to know

Check recommended vaccinations and prophylaxis with your doctor or a travel clinic before leaving. At 2,400 m take the first days easy.
