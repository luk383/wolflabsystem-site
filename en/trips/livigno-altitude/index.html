Livigno

Altitude · run, bike, swimValtellina, Italy
When
June to September
Altitude
1,816 m in town, up to over 3,000 m

Why go

Italy's reference destination for summer altitude camps: the Aquagranda centre has a 6-lane athletics track, 25 and 50 m pools and a gym, close to trails and Alpine passes.

What to train

Running on trails and the valley cycle path, track sessions, bike climbs towards Foscagno, Eira and Bernina, swimming for recovery.

Costs

Duty-free zone: fuel and some purchases cost less. In summer book accommodation early, especially in August.

Getting there

By road only: from Italy via Bormio and the Foscagno pass, from Switzerland via the Munt La Schera tunnel. Most convenient airports: Milan and Bergamo.

Good to know

Go easier for the first days at altitude and drink more. Blood adaptations to altitude generally need at least three weeks; a one-week camp is mostly about volume and motivation.
