Sierra Nevada

Altitude · run and bikeGranada, Spain
When
May to October
Altitude
High-performance centre at about 2,300 m; Granada at about 700 m

Why go

Spain's high-performance centre has hosted international middle-distance runners, swimmers and cyclists for years, with lower altitudes close by for fast sessions.

What to train

Running on high-altitude dirt roads, one of Europe's longest bike climbs towards Pico Veleta, faster work lower down.

Costs

Places at the centre are limited and often reserved for federations and teams: check availability well ahead; otherwise ski-resort hotels in summer.

Getting there

Granada (GRX) or Malaga (AGP) airport, then about an hour of mountain road from Granada.

Good to know

At altitude sun and wind are strong even in summer. As with all altitude camps, take the first days easy.
