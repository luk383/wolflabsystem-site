Trips

Tenerife and Mount Teide

Altitude · bike and runCanary Islands, Spain
When
All year; busiest from January to April
Altitude
Las Cañadas Parador at over 2,000 m above sea level (per the Parador); coast at sea level

Why go

You can sleep high and drop down to train low, with long, steady climbs and mild winter weather. It's one of the favourite altitude camp destinations for pro teams.

What to train

Cycling volume on 30–40 km climbs, easy running and long runs on the volcanic plateau, harder work down towards the coast.

Costs

Flights from many European cities; the Parador has few rooms and must be booked months ahead. Bike hire available in Los Cristianos, Adeje and Puerto de la Cruz.

Getting there

Tenerife South (TFS) and Tenerife North (TFN) airports; you need a car to reach the Parador.

Good to know

There are no shops at altitude: carry food and bottles. The sun is strong even in winter and nights get cold.
