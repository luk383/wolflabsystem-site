Trips

Mallorca by bike

BikeBalearic Islands, Spain
When
February to May and autumn
Altitude
Sea level; Serra de Tramuntana climbs

Why go

Well-kept roads, flats for volume and mountains a few kilometres away, with an infrastructure built for cyclists: rentals, hotels with bike storage, guided groups.

What to train

Long flat rides in the centre of the island and Tramuntana climbs, such as Sa Calobra and Puig Major, for climbing endurance.

Costs

Carbon road bike hire: about €190–300 per week (€50–70 per day) depending on the model, in a major rental's 2026 price list.

Getting there

Palma airport (PMI), with many direct flights from Italy in spring.

Good to know

In March and April the roads are full of cyclists: book bike and hotel early. The Mallorca 312 sportive also takes place in spring.
