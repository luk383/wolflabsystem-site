Zwift Cog & Click and the Kickr Core 2 bundles: what they are, compatibility and list prices

Materials3 min readPublished November 21, 2026

  2. Which trainers it works with
  3. The bundles and the list prices
  4. Limits

At a glance

  • The Zwift Cog & Click kit adds 24-gear virtual shifting and handlebar controls (shifting, steering, menus, Ride On) to an existing compatible smart trainer, with tool-free installation. Official list price: 49.99 euros.12
  • Compatibility is not universal: the official page points to a growing list of "Zwift Cog Compatible" trainers to select by brand and model, and the kit does not work with Shimano Micro Spline, SRAM XD/XDR or Campagnolo freehubs or with 1/8" chains (fixed gear and singlespeed).2
  • If you are starting from scratch, Zwift sells the Wahoo Kickr Core 2 trainer already bundled with Cog and Click (list price 549.99 euros) and the Zwift Ride smart bike with the Kickr Core 2 included (list price 1,199.99 euros; the Zwift Ride Smart Frame alone stays at 799.99 euros).1
  • The Click works with both Zwift and Rouvy. The platform subscription remains a separate cost from the hardware.12

What the Cog & Click kit does

The Cog is a single-speed cassette that, paired with the Click controller on the handlebar, enables virtual shifting: 24 "silent, instant" gears, according to the manufacturer, with no lateral chain movement. The Click puts Zwift controls (shifting, steering, menus, Ride On) on the handlebar and, after a recent update, also works with Rouvy. Installation is described as quick and tool-free.2

According to the official product page the kit is compatible with almost any 8-to-13-speed bike, but not with Shimano Micro Spline, SRAM XD/XDR or Campagnolo freehubs, nor with 1/8" chains typical of fixed-gear and singlespeed bikes. These are manufacturer claims, not our verification on real bikes.2

Which trainers it works with

Trainer compatibility is the point to check first: the official page speaks of "a growing list of Zwift Cog Compatible smart trainers" and requires selecting the brand and model of your trainer from a menu — among the listed brands are Elite, Garmin Tacx, JetBlack, Van Rysel, Wahoo and Zwift, with model-specific kit versions. Some trainers have particular limits: the page notes, for example, that Tacx Neo 2 units are compatible only with a specific axle type, pointing to Garmin documentation. If your model is not listed, the site invites you to contact support.2

The bundles and the list prices

On the official European shop, the list prices (shown by Zwift as "Regular price") verified on 23 November 2026 are: 49.99 euros for the Cog & Click Upgrade Kit, 549.99 euros for the Wahoo Kickr Core 2 trainer bundled with Zwift Cog and Click, and 1,199.99 euros for the Zwift Ride smart bike with the Kickr Core 2 included. The Zwift Ride Smart Frame alone is listed at 799.99 euros.12

The purchase covers hardware only: the Zwift subscription (or another platform's) remains a separate, recurring cost. Shipping, availability for Italy and returns are confirmed at checkout, not on the product page.1
