2026 Aquathlon Worlds: gold for Perham and Riasova in Abu Dhabi, Maksimava fourth a day after gold

In brief

  • Chris Perham (GBR) is the new aquathlon world champion: 30:27 over the run-swim-run course on Hudayriyat Island, decided by a burst one kilometre from the line. Silver for Kevin Tarek Viñuela (ESP) at 10 seconds, bronze and the junior title for Damian Suarez Couto (ESP, born 2007) at 11.12
  • Valentina Riasova (RUS) takes women's gold in 34:37: her teammate Iana Chenskaia took a wrong turn in transition and served a 10-second penalty, being passed just as the stop ran out. Silver for Margot Garabedian (CAM) at 3 seconds, bronze and the U23 title for Chenskaia at 4.12
  • Hanna Maksimava (BLR), yesterday's cross duathlon champion, finishes fourth at 24 seconds. No Italian elite starters: 21 finishers and 3 disqualified in the men's race, 25 finishers in the women's.2
  • Official conditions: 26.1 °C water, 25.6 °C air, wetsuits forbidden. The Multisport Worlds close on Sunday 22 November with the Long Distance race.23

The format and the conditions

The 2026 Aquathlon World Championships took place on Friday 20 November on the beach of Hudayriyat Island, Abu Dhabi, on the final day of short-format racing at the Multisport World Championships, with elite, U23, junior, para and age-group titles on offer. The elite format per the official results page: a 2.7 km run, a 1 km sea swim and a 2.6 km final run. Men started at 9:00 local time, women at 9:05. Conditions recorded at the start: 26.1 °C water, 25.6 °C air, wetsuits forbidden.2

Men's race: Perham picks his moment

Perham stayed with the lead group through the first run alongside Spain's Jonay Alvarez and Viñuela, led a front five out of the water and, after a laboured start to the final run as he struggled into his shoes, made the decisive attack one kilometre from the finish: nobody responded. "I picked a spot I wanted to go before the race, went early, wanted to catch people by surprise and it worked. The swim is my strongest part and only having a 2.5km run afterwards meant it was likely to stick," he said, recalling the specific heat preparation done back in Nottingham.1

Behind him, Viñuela held on to silver by ten seconds over Suarez Couto, who at 19 takes elite bronze and the junior world title; the U23 crown goes to Semen Kashitsyn (RUS), seventh overall at 40 seconds. Four Russians in the top seven. Also of note: three disqualifications for unserved penalties, including Alvarez, who had been at the front in the opening run and leaves without a classification.12

Women's race: the penalty that decided gold

The turning point came in transition: Chenskaia took a wrong turn towards the beach and collected a 10-second penalty to be served late in the race. Riasova entered the water in front, clear of Dugovičová and Maksimava, while Garabedian, some thirty seconds back, swam her way back into contention. On the final run Chenskaia stopped to serve the penalty without enough of a cushion: Riasova and Garabedian passed her just as the count expired. Gold for Riasova in 34:37, silver for the Cambodian three seconds back, bronze and the U23 title for Chenskaia at four.12

Fourth was Hanna Maksimava at 24 seconds: the Belarusian was back racing less than 24 hours after winning the cross duathlon world title on the same island yesterday. Fifth place for Laura Dugovičová (SVK, born 2009), who takes the junior title; sixth for Marina Muñoz Hernando (ESP).12

No Italians, and what remains of the programme

The official classification records no Italian starters in the elite races: 21 finishers and 3 disqualified out of 24 starters among the men, 25 finishers out of 25 among the women. After cross triathlon, cross duathlon and aquathlon, the Abu Dhabi Multisport World Championships close on Sunday 22 November with the Long Distance race, the last title on offer according to the event's official page.23

Limitations

This is a report based on World Triathlon's race chronicle and the official classification published on the federation's site, not on our presence at the venue. The official distances recorded on the results page (2.7 km + 1 km + 2.6 km) differ from the 2.5 km runs mentioned in World Triathlon's chronicle: we use the classification figure. The dynamics of Chenskaia's penalty (wrong turn and being passed as the 10 seconds expired) are described by the official chronicle alone; Chenskaia's final time in the classification includes the penalty, but the page does not break it down. The U23 and junior titles follow from combining the chronicle with the elite classification, which includes those categories. The para and age-group races in the same programme are not covered by this article. The Sunday 22 November date for the Long Distance race was verified on the event's official page on 20 November.
