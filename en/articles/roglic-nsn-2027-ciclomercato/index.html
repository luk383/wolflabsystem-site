Roglič joins Iniesta's NSN: a one-year deal for the final chapter, and what it means for the transfer market

Cycling4 min readPublished November 21, 2026
On this page

  2. What NSN is
  3. The weight of the move
  4. Limitations

In brief

  • Primož Roglič is a new NSN Cycling Team rider: a one-year contract for the 2027 season, announced on 18 November on the team's official channels. He leaves Red Bull-Bora-hansgrohe after two years.13
  • NSN is a Swiss-Spanish WorldTour team born from the collaboration between Never Say Never, the company co-founded by Andrés Iniesta, and Swiss investment group Stoneweg. In 2027 it will also field fellow newcomers Richard Carapaz, Santiago Buitrago and Lennert Van Eetvelt.12
  • The Slovenian's record in the official announcement: four Vuelta a España titles, one Giro d'Italia, Olympic gold and 93 wins in 11 years as a professional. At 37 he speaks of a "final chapter" and of challenges still to take on.1
  • The 2027 race programme remains an open question: Sky Sport floats the Giro d'Italia, whose next edition features demanding stages like the double Mortirolo but also many time-trial kilometres, ideal terrain for the Slovenian.3

The announcement

NSN Cycling Team made it official on 18 November: Primož Roglič has signed a one-year contract for 2027, described by the team as the arrival of "one of professional cycling's most successful and recognizable riders". The announcement's headline speaks of a "final chapter": the 37-year-old Slovenian arrives after two seasons with Red Bull-Bora-hansgrohe.13

In his first words: "I always like challenges. That's why I'm here, to take these on together and see what the future brings us. It took me some time to figure out how to make a chapter in my life where I am peaceful, but the answer is actually simple with the team name: Never Say Never." General manager Kjell Carlström: "From the first moment we spoke, it was evident that Primož still has a lot of passion for the sport and a drive to be the best he can. It was an easy decision to make."1

What NSN is

NSN Cycling Team races in the WorldTour under a Swiss licence with its operational base in Spain: it was born from the collaboration between NSN (Never Say Never), the global sports and events company co-founded by Andrés Iniesta, and Stoneweg, an investment group headquartered in Geneva. Across the WorldTeam and Development Team it counts around 50 riders; the roster includes Biniam Girmay, Ethan Vernon and George Bennett. The 2026 season ended with 16 wins.2

For 2027 the team had already announced three more major signings alongside Roglič: Richard Carapaz, Santiago Buitrago and Lennert Van Eetvelt, a line-up clearly aimed at Grand Tour general classification ambitions.1

The weight of the move

Four Vuelta a España titles, one Giro d'Italia, Olympic gold and 93 career wins: the record cited in the announcement explains why Roglič's future was one of the transfer market's open questions. His departure from Red Bull-Bora-hansgrohe was made official at the end of the season.13

The next question concerns the calendar: Sky Sport notes that the 2027 Giro d'Italia will feature severe stages like the double Mortirolo but also many time-trial kilometres, characteristics that suit the Slovenian, who won the Giro in 2023. For now, though, there is no official confirmation of Roglič's programme: it remains a hypothesis.3

Limitations

This article is based on NSN Cycling Team's official announcement, the team's information page and Sky Sport's coverage. Contract length and career record are those stated by the team. The general classification ambitions described are the intentions expressed by the parties, not a guarantee of results. The 2027 Giro hypothesis comes from the cited press coverage and is not confirmed by the team or the rider. Financial details of the deal are not public. Andrés Iniesta's exact role in sporting management is not detailed by the sources: the team describes him as co-founder of NSN.
