HYROX and World Triathlon: what changes for runners, as FITRI opens 2027 affiliations

In short

  • On 28 October World Triathlon and HYROX signed a term sheet: Hybrid Racing (running plus functional workout stations) becomes a recognised discipline within the World Triathlon family, across three formats. The definitive operational agreement (Long Form Agreement) has yet to be signed.1
  • HYROX will be the exclusive operator of the official World, Continental and National Championships for elite athletes in the Ultimate format; national federations will be able to organise events in all three formats, including Age Group National Championships.1
  • In Italy the shift is already concrete: FITRI published its 2027 Hybrid Racing affiliation circular on 13 November, and from today, 18 November, clubs can apply. Affiliation €150, athlete card €15, categories from Youth B (16-17) to Master 10, triathlon-specific competitive medical certificate required.2
  • The figures quoted in recent days (2.5 million participants and around 135 events planned by HYROX for 2026-27) are the organiser's forecasts as reported by the Gazzetta, not actuals. Anti-doping and the full regulatory framework will be set in the definitive agreement.6

What was signed (and what was not)

On 28 October World Triathlon, the international federation for triathlon and multisport, and HYROX, the global functional fitness racing series, announced the signing of a term sheet launching a strategic partnership. The wording matters: a term sheet is the framework understanding, not the definitive operational agreement. World Triathlon itself writes that a Long Form Agreement will follow, "that will set out the full operational, governance and commercial framework of the partnership". Some recent headlines speak of a "historic agreement signed": that phrasing goes beyond the primary document.16

What the term sheet establishes: Hybrid Racing, the format combining running with functional workouts, becomes a recognised discipline within the World Triathlon family and will be contested across three formats: Ultimate, mirroring the current HYROX race format; Fast, shorter and higher-intensity; and Ultra, extended and endurance-based. All three will be sanctioned and governed by World Triathlon and developed jointly with HYROX. The stated goal is a shared pathway toward Olympic and Paralympic recognition.1

Who organises what

HYROX will act as the exclusive operator of the official World, Continental and National Championships for elite athletes in the Ultimate format, integrating them into its existing elite competition structure. National federations, more than 177 in the World Triathlon network, will have the opportunity to organise Hybrid Racing events across all three formats, including National Championships for Age Group athletes, meaning amateurs. This is the part that matters most to runners: the institutional entry point will not be only the private circuit, but also national triathlon federations.1

The road so far, briefly

At the October 2025 Congress in Wollongong, member federations voted by an overwhelming majority to approve two new disciplines under the World Triathlon umbrella: Fitness Racing, the format popularised by HYROX (eight 1-km running segments interspersed with eight functional stations), and Swimrun. In the following months the discipline was renamed Hyathlon: in April 2026 World Triathlon confirmed its approval following the agreement with HYROX, with competition rules being finalised. The Italian federation's 2027 documents use the wording "Hybrid Racing".45

What changes in Italy: the FITRI circular and 2027 affiliations

FITRI moved early: in January 2026 it called an extraordinary national assembly to add the three disciplines approved at Wollongong (Aquabike, Swimrun and Hyathlon) to its statute.3 On 13 November it then published the circular "New Hybrid Racing Affiliations 2027" with the handbook and membership forms. The key point: from today, 18 November 2026, Italian sports clubs (ASD/SSD) can submit the documentation to affiliate with FITRI for Hybrid Racing in the 2027 season. Applications can be filed at any time of year; clubs affiliated for Hybrid Racing may carry out only that activity.2

The fees in the circular's Table A: €150 for a new Hybrid Racing affiliation (covering one coach, one medic and membership for five board members) and €15 per athlete card, from the Youth B category (16-17 years) up to Master 10. Competitive activity requires a triathlon-specific competitive medical certificate; insurance cover is provided by FITRI from the completion of membership, the card is virtual via the MyFITRI app (the physical badge has been abolished) and membership is valid for the calendar year. Registered athletes can enter events on the federal calendar as long as membership is completed before entries close.2

In practice, for a runner who currently races HYROX as a private entrant, the novelty is a federal channel: affiliated clubs, competitive membership, age-group categories and, in prospect, Italian Age Group Championships organised by the federation, as the term sheet envisages for every national federation.12

The numbers and the unknowns

According to the Gazzetta dello Sport, HYROX expects 2.5 million participants and 2.89 million spectators across around 135 events in the 2026-27 season: these are the organiser's forecasts, useful to grasp the scale of the phenomenon but to be treated as such. The same report notes that anti-doping aspects are to be clarified in the definitive agreement, that triathlon at Los Angeles 2028 is unchanged (individual events and the Mixed Relay), and that in December the IOC will announce which candidate new disciplines remain in the running for Brisbane 2032. The optimistic statements about reaching the Olympics, in the primary source, come from HYROX's co-founders and the World Triathlon president: interested parties, not independent assessments.61

Limits

The signed document is a term sheet: contents, governance, commercial and anti-doping aspects will be defined in the Long Form Agreement, not yet published. The discipline's competition rules are still being finalised. The participant and event figures for 2026-27 are the organiser's forecasts as reported by the press. The FITRI fees quoted come from the 2027 affiliation circular published on 13 November and may be updated by the federation; this article is no substitute for reading the full circular for anyone intending to affiliate a club or register as an athlete.
