In brief

  • Sunday 15 November 2026 is the 25th Eurospin Verona Run Marathon: half marathon at 8:00, marathon at 9:00, both from Corso Porta Nuova (Piazza Bra), finishing on the Liston. Family Run at 11:15.345
  • The passage inside the Arena amphitheatre, in the last kilometre of the 42K and 21K, is confirmed: general director Stefano Stanzial announced it on 12 November after a week of uncertainty. It does not apply to the Family Run.1
  • Bib pickup at the Expo Village (Pala Magis): Friday 13 from 11:00 to 20:00, Saturday 14 from 10:00 to 20:00, Sunday 15 only from 6:30 to 8:00 (until 9:30 for the Family Run).6
  • Online registration closed on 11 November; at the Expo, on Friday and Saturday only, you can still register for 109 euros if places remain. No registration on Sunday.7

The Arena passage: confirmed on 12 November

The 25th edition of the Verona Run Marathon will do something never done before: the marathon and half marathon will run inside the Arena amphitheatre during the last kilometre, shortly before the finish on the Liston in Piazza Bra.1

Confirmation came on 12 November through a direct statement from general director Stefano Stanzial, who thanked the offices involved for completing the authorisation process for the Arena passage. In the previous days the specialist press had reported doubts and a wait for the permit; the passage had first been announced in early October. The administrative document is not public and we have not seen it: the confirmation is the director's press statement.1

The race website still warns that the course may undergo slight variations based on communications from the authorities.2

Before the start: expo, bibs and registration

The Expo Village is at Pala Magis (PalaOlimpia), Piazzale Atleti Azzurri d'Italia 1: open Friday 13 November from 11:00 to 20:00, Saturday 14 from 10:00 to 20:00. On Sunday 15 it opens only for bib and race-kit pickup, from 6:30 to 8:00 (until 9:30 for the Family Run).6

To collect your bib you need the digital confirmation letter on your smartphone and an ID document; pickup by proxy is possible with the required documents.6

Online registration closed on 11 November. Anyone still out can register at the Expo Village on Friday 13 and Saturday 14 for 109 euros, if places remain: no registration is taken on Sunday.7

Start times and course

All races start from Corso Porta Nuova, near Piazza Bra: the half marathon (21.097 km) at 8:00, the marathon (42.195 km) at 9:00, the Family Run at 11:15. Marathon and half marathon finish on the Liston in Piazza Bra; the Family Run ends in Piazzetta Mura Galieno, behind the Arena, and does not enter the amphitheatre.3451

The marathon start is split into corrals assigned on your bib: the organiser asks runners to enter their corral at least 20 minutes before the gun and warns that starting from an unassigned corral means exclusion from the results. The marathon time limit is 6 hours; bags go to the trucks near the start, with the sticker applied, by 8:30.3

Course novelties this year: the first passage over the Castelvecchio bridge around kilometre 18 and the one inside Porta Vescovo at kilometre 40; the passage through the G. Duca barracks in San Martino Buon Albergo, already on the marathon course, includes the half marathon for the first time.1

A free continuous shuttle links the C Stadio car park (expo area), Porta Nuova station (stop E2) and the start area: Saturday 14 from 9:00 to 20:00 and Sunday 15 from 6:30 to 16:00.3

The technical side: women's record under threat

The men's course record is 2:09:16 by Kenya's Albert Matebor Kiplagat (2011); the women's is 2:29:09 by Italy's Giovanna Epis, set here in 2023 with the national title. According to the eve-of-race technical presentation, the women's record is a stated target: the favourite is Ethiopia's Genet Yalew Kassahun (2:24:34 in Prague 2019) with a dedicated pacemaker, Luca Parisi. Among the men the pick is Kenya's Mathew Kibiwot Sang (2:09:20 in Malaga 2022).1

The organiser announces about 15,000 participants across the three races, with thousands from abroad; the race is a Fidal Silver Label event and sits on the AIMS and World Athletics calendars.1

Weather: cold and dry, check again

The forecast for Verona on Sunday 15 November (Open-Meteo model, read on 12 November) shows a low of 4 °C, a high of 11 °C, an 18% probability of rain and maximum wind of 7 km/h. It is a three-day forecast: check it again the day before.8

Limits

  • The Arena passage confirmation is the general director's press statement republished on 12 November by Tuttosport, which is a media partner of the event: the administrative document (permit) is not public and we have not seen it.
  • Participation numbers (about 15,000), record expectations and elite-field details come from the eve-of-race presentation reported by the press: they are organiser data unless stated otherwise.
  • The expo registration fee (109 euros) applies if places remain, according to the race website.
  • The weather is a model forecast read on 12 November and can change.
