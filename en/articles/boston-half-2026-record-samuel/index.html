Boston Half 2026: Samuel runs 59:49, first sub-hour half marathon in Massachusetts

In short

  • On 8 November 2026 Habtom Samuel (Eritrea) won the Boston Half in 59:49: an event record and the first sub-hour half marathon run in Massachusetts.
  • In the women's race Fentaye Belayneh (Ethiopia) won in 1:09:14, the first athlete with three consecutive victories in the event.
  • Times and facts come from the official B.A.A. release and a concordant news report; full results are public on the organiser's portal.

Men's race

Samuel broke away from the pack shortly after 15K and finished alone in 59:49, shaving 45 seconds off the event record (1:00:34) set in 2013 by Lelisa Desisa, a two-time Boston Marathon champion.1,2

  1. Habtom Samuel (ERI): 59:49, event record
  2. Andrea Kiptoo (KEN): 1:01:03
  3. Abel Kipchumba (KEN): 1:01:16

Jack Aho was sixth and top American in 1:01:53, the fastest time by a US athlete in event history (previous best 1:01:57 by Chandler Gibbens in 2025).1

Women's race

Belayneh settled the race in a sprint on the final hill into Franklin Park, finishing two seconds ahead of Kenya's Winfred Mbithe (1:09:16). Ethiopia's Sintayehu Lewetegn was third in 1:09:26.1

  1. Fentaye Belayneh (ETH): 1:09:14
  2. Winfred Mbithe (KEN): 1:09:16
  3. Sintayehu Lewetegn (ETH): 1:09:26

With this win Belayneh is the first woman to take three consecutive Boston Half titles. Sydney Vaught was fifth and top American in 1:09:44, in her debut at the distance.1

Wheelchair divisions

Daniel Romanchuk (USA) won the men's wheelchair division in 45:53, a new event record: 1:46 faster than his own 2024 mark. In the women's race Yen Hoang (USA) edged Jenna Ayers in a photo finish: both were credited with 1:02:54.1

Context

The race started and finished in Franklin Park, Boston, with more than 7,400 participants; over 600 Team Dana-Farber runners raised nearly $1 million. Figures stated by the organiser.1 According to LetsRun, the two winners earned $15,000 each, with a $10,000 record bonus for Samuel.2
