Turin and Florence marathons on 29 November: online registration closed and race programme

Running5 min readPublished November 18, 2026
On this page
  1. Turin: online closed, Village information
  2. Florence: separate sell-out notices from deadlines
  3. Limitations and update
  4. Sources

At a glance

  • Torino City Marathon and the 42nd Estra Firenze Marathon take place on Sunday 29 November 2026.
  • At our 23 November check, Turin lists online registration as closed, with Village registration on 27-28 November. The first version's online deadline of 20 November has passed.1
  • Florence: the organiser announced a 42K sell-out. The published ordinary 10K deadline was 19 November and has passed. We have not checked exceptions or remaining entries on ENDU.2

Turin: online closed, Village information

The official homepage, read on 23 November, says online registration is closed and lists registration at the Green Pea Marathon Village on 27-28 November. We no longer present the fee valid until 20 November as a current option.1

The race document published on FIDAL's website schedules the start for 08:30 on 29 November in Piazza Vittorio Veneto and the finish in Piazza Castello, subject to organisational requirements. Bib collection is listed at GreenPea, Via Ermanno Fenoglietti 20, on Friday 27 and Saturday 28, from 10:00 to 20:00. Follow your personal confirmation letter and any later organiser notices too.3

Florence: separate sell-out notices from deadlines

The official 27 August announcement reports a sold-out 42K with 13,000 entries and lists 19 November as the ordinary deadline for the 10K, subject to its 3,000-entry cap being reached earlier. That date must no longer be presented as a future deadline. The 10K page confirms the 29 November programme, with an 08:45 start in Via Tornabuoni; it does not itself establish that registration has reopened.24

Limitations and update

23 November 2026 update: the original 18 November title and summary presented Turin's online registration and Florence's 10K window as still open. Those deadlines have passed. We have updated the title, summary and practical details while keeping the original publication date. We have not checked remaining entries on ENDU or tourist packages. We do not quote a current Turin fee: the homepage and the FIDAL race document consulted show different Village amounts, so confirm the cost with the organiser before paying.
