FTP and power zones: what they measure and how far to trust them

In short

  • FTP is the power you can hold for about an hour. The 20-minute test estimates it by taking 95%.
  • It's an approximation: in studies the value can differ from lab-measured threshold, with large differences between people.
  • Use it to set your zones, then check them against feel and heart rate.

What FTP measures

FTP stands for Functional Threshold Power: the highest power you can sustain steadily for about an hour. The concept was popularised by Hunter Allen and Andrew Coggan, who also proposed training zones as percentages of FTP.1

Physiologically, the limit that matters is the maximal metabolic steady state: the highest intensity at which lactate and oxygen uptake stay stable. Scientists measure it with critical power or lactate tests.2 FTP is a practical way to get close to that value without a lab., but it does not measure it.

Studies of the 20-minute test are few and mostly on trained male cyclists.5 The test is repeatable: in 19 highly trained athletes two tests gave similar results (difference between −17 and +13 W).3 But repeatable does not mean exact: in 18 well-trained people, power at maximal lactate steady state (MLSS) was on average 88.5% of 20-minute FTP and 93% of the 95%-adjusted FTP, so the 95% overestimated it; in 23 cyclists the 95% of 20 minutes was close to individual threshold on average, but individuals differed by up to ±40–60 W.46 These are small samples.

The 20-minute test

  1. Warm up for 15–20 minutes with a couple of short efforts.
  2. 5 minutes hard, then 10 minutes easy, to use up your anaerobic reserve.
  3. 20 minutes at the highest average power you can hold, starting controlled.
  4. Estimated FTP = 20-minute average power × 0.95.

The 0.95 factor is a convention: it gives different results from person to person and in some studies overestimates threshold. Treat it as a starting point.

The zones, simplified

Zone% FTPUse
Recoverybelow 55%Easy recovery rides
Endurance56–75%The base: long, easy rides
Tempo76–90%Sustained pace, in measured doses
Threshold91–105%8–20 minute blocks
VO₂max106–120%3–5 minute intervals

The zones and retest interval are the Allen and Coggan convention, not results of controlled studies.

Repeat the test every 6–8 weeks, or when your zones clearly feel too easy or too hard.
