  • The official Watch S5 41mm page confirms Google Pay and Xiaomi Pay, subject to region and bank support; both devices broadcast heart rate over Bluetooth to bike computers and third-party apps. This article includes no independent device tests.713

Watch S5 41mm: what arrives

Stainless steel case, 41.4 mm, 9.9 mm thick and 36 g without the strap. The 1.32-inch AMOLED display (466 × 466 pixels, 352 PPI) claims a 3,000-nit peak on a limited area and 1,500 nits HBM across the full screen. The system is HyperOS 4, not Wear OS.12

The Italian page confirms Google Pay and Xiaomi Pay payments; its notes also name the Google Wallet trademark. The official page notes that availability depends on region and bank.781

For the outdoors: built-in GNSS with five satellite systems (GPS, Galileo, GLONASS, BeiDou, QZSS), offline maps downloaded through the app and route navigation. 5 ATM water resistance under ISO 22810, with underwater heart-rate monitoring.271

The battery is 590 mAh. The claimed "up to 14 days" applies to Xiaomi's light-usage scenario: heart rate every 10 minutes, continuous stress and blood-oxygen monitoring off, 90 minutes of GNSS workouts per week.18

On the sensor: four LEDs and four photodiodes with a proprietary algorithm. The claimed 98.4% accuracy has a precise definition in the official notes: the share of readings with an absolute error under 10 bpm versus a reference standard, measured in Xiaomi's internal labs across scenarios including indoor and outdoor running and cycling. It is not a medical figure: the reference standard is not identified in the note read, so we do not present it as a chest-strap comparison.1

Smart Band 11: the dual sensor and claimed battery life

The band is 9.99 mm thick and weighs 15.58 g without the strap (21.45 g for the Ceramic Edition). The 1.72-inch AMOLED display (212 × 520 pixels) has symmetrical 2 mm bezels and a 60 Hz refresh rate.34

The heart-rate sensor uses two LEDs and two photodiodes. The official page describes more precise monitoring but does not provide independent validation here. The band is not a medical device.3

The official battery life of the 233 mAh cell is stated per scenario: 21 days in light use (heart rate every 30 minutes, continuous stress and blood-oxygen monitoring off), 10 days in normal use and 6 days with the always-on display. The Italian page read does not split these figures by version, so we do not automatically extend them to every NFC variant.38

Among the features: 150+ sport modes, underwater heart rate, dual connection to a Xiaomi phone and an iPhone (requires HyperOS 3 or later on the Xiaomi phone, iOS 14 or later on the iPhone, compatible versions of the Xiaomi Interconnectivity app and the same Xiaomi account on both devices) and contactless payments on the NFC versions, subject to bank support.36

For running, cycling and swimming

The most relevant detail for training is shared by both devices: they broadcast real-time heart rate over Bluetooth to bike computers and third-party apps that support the BLE protocol. For indoor or road cycling they can feed heart-rate data to the head unit without a dedicated strap; accuracy in this mode has not yet been verified by independent tests.13

For running, the Watch S5 41mm is the more complete of the two: multi-satellite GNSS, offline maps with no continuous-GNSS battery measurement verified in this article. For swimming, both claim 5 ATM and underwater heart rate, with the usual limits: the rating covers pools and calm open water, not hot showers or diving, and leather or fabric straps should not go in the water.23

The prudent advice is the same as on any launch day: the sources read do not provide independent measurements of GNSS accuracy, heart rate during activity or real battery life. Before replacing a chest strap or a dedicated watch, it is worth waiting for the first instrumented reviews.

Limitations

WOLFLAB has not tested Watch S5 41mm or Smart Band 11. Every number quoted is claimed by the manufacturer or reported by the launch coverage; the Watch's 98.4% figure comes from Xiaomi's labs using the definition above, not our own validation against a chest strap or medical instrument. The prices reported are the official list prices verified on mi.com on 23 November 2026; the Smart Band 11 Ceramic and Unibody Edition, although announced at €89.99, were not selectable on the Italian buy page at that date. The availability of Google Wallet, Xiaomi Pay and NFC payments depends on region and bank. Update of 23 November: the first version of this article also reported the launch promotional prices observed on mi.com on 17 November, alongside the list prices. Under our editorial policy we publish only official list prices until WOLFLAB has active affiliate programmes: promotional prices have been removed, and the technical content is unchanged.
