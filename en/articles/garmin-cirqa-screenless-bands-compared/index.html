Garmin CIRQA and the screenless alternatives

In short

  • CIRQA Smart Band is Garmin's first screen-free smart band, announced on 21 July 2026. No subscription, up to 10 days of claimed battery life, no built-in GPS.
  • Price: 199.99 € in the European listings I read, 199.99 $ in the United States.
  • The alternatives differ mainly on cost: Whoop needs an annual membership, Fitbit Air and Amazfit Helio Strap cost about half, Polar Loop is close to CIRQA.
  • This compares manufacturer-stated data only.

What CIRQA is

According to Garmin it is a fabric-band smart band with no display that records wrist heart rate, Body Battery, Pulse Ox, stress, skin temperature, sleep with stages and HRV. Data is read in the Garmin Connect app. It detects activities automatically and lets you start more than 80 activities manually with the single side button. GPS comes from the connected smartphone.1

The manual lists up to 10 days of battery and 5 ATM water resistance.2 Garmin says no subscription is needed. The US list price is 199.99 $.1 In Europe I found 199.99 € on Garmin Greece and at Italian retailers; I did not verify an official Garmin Italy list price.3

What changes in practice

  • Garmin CIRQA makes most sense if you already use a Garmin watch: data goes to Garmin Connect with the same metrics, including readiness, VO2 max and training status. It does not record your route on its own: it needs the phone.1
  • Polar Loop is the closest in business model: no subscription, one-time purchase, free Polar Flow app. WR30 water resistance and about 8 days of battery.4
  • Whoop 5.0 works only with the membership: One is 199 € a year, Peak 264, Life 399.5 Over time it costs more than the others. It has 14+ days of battery and, Whoop says, no display or built-in GPS.6
  • Fitbit Air starts at 99.99 €, with a three-month Google Health Premium trial. The coach requires Premium.7
  • Amazfit Helio Strap costs 99.90 €, with 5 ATM, up to 10 days and a free Zepp app.8

How to choose

Three questions are enough. Do you already have a Garmin watch? Then CIRQA fits in at no extra cost. Do you want to avoid any subscription? Rule out Whoop, and remember that the Fitbit Air coach requires Premium. Looking for the lowest price? Fitbit Air and Helio Strap cost about half. The rest, comfort, sensor accuracy and app quality, depends on use.

Limits

  • Heart-rate and sleep accuracy and comfort are not assessed.
  • Polar Loop and Fitbit Air prices come from launch releases (Polar: September 2025; Google: May 2026); the current price in Italy may differ.
  • Whoop: the price is the membership with the sensor included, not a separate purchase; the figure comes from Whoop's release on retailers in Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium and from Italian press.
  • GPS, water resistance and features of Polar, Fitbit Air and Amazfit are not all compared: we report only what the sources state.
  • The starting point was a The Running Club review, used only as a tip: the data in this article comes from the manufacturers.
