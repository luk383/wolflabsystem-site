Milano21 2026: Musau wins in 1:01:31, Hassan Nour dominates the women's race. Chiappinelli sixth on his comeback

Multisport4 min readPublished November 22, 2026
On this page
  1. Musau holds off Irakoze and Heikkilä
  2. Hassan Nour unchallenged, Epis first Italian
  3. Loss and Colli take the 10K
  4. Limitations
  5. Sources

In brief

  • Titus Musau (UGA) won the Milano21 Half Marathon 2026 in 1:01:31, with Burundi's Irakoze 3 seconds back and Finland's Heikkilä 10 behind. The race was run in pouring rain and cold, with 20,000 runners on the start line.12
  • First Italian home was Yohanes Chiappinelli, sixth in 1:03:50 on his comeback from a long injury; Riccardo Martellato (8th, 1:04:01) and Samuel Medolago (9th, 1:04:12) also made the top 10.12
  • The women's race was dominated by Samiyah Hassan Nour (DJI), on her debut at the distance: 1:08:04 and more than two minutes clear of Kenyans Eva Cherono (1:10:11) and Regina Wambui Ndungu (1:10:41). Giovanna Epis was sixth in 1:13:04, first Italian, with Aurora Bado eighth in 1:14:19.12
  • Italian double in the 10K: Nikolas Loss won the men's race in 30:18, steeplechaser Gaia Colli the women's in 33:57.2

Musau holds off Irakoze and Heikkilä

The Milan half marathon was run in wintry conditions, with driving rain and cold, but that did not stop the party: according to Runner's World, 20,000 runners started, with athletes from 125 nations. Up front in the men's race, Uganda's Titus Musau finished in 1:01:31, with Burundi's Irakoze just 3 seconds behind and Finland's Heikkilä 10 seconds back.12 Uganda's Alex Kiplangat was fourth in 1:01:54 and Eritrea's Abderezak Suleman fifth in 1:03:49.3

Sixth overall and first Italian was Yohanes Chiappinelli in 1:03:50, on his comeback from a long injury according to Runner's World. Also in the top 10 were Riccardo Martellato, eighth in 1:04:01, and Samuel Medolago, ninth in 1:04:12.12

Hassan Nour unchallenged, Epis first Italian

In the women's race, Djibouti's Samiyah Hassan Nour, making her half marathon debut, won by more than two minutes in 1:08:04. Behind her came Kenyans Eva Cherono, second in 1:10:11, and Regina Wambui Ndungu, third in 1:10:41.12

Giovanna Epis, coming off the New York City Marathon, was the first Italian: sixth in 1:13:04. Aurora Bado also made the top 10, eighth in 1:14:19.2

Loss and Colli take the 10K

An Italian double in the 10 kilometres: in the men's race Nikolas Loss won in 30:18 ahead of Andrea Elia (30:54) and René Cuneaz (30:57); in the women's, steeplechaser Gaia Colli finished in 33:57, ahead of Bulgaria's Bilserin Syuleyman (34:13) and Switzerland's Giulia Fulginiti (35:00).2

Limitations

This is a results piece based on two independent journalistic reports (OA Sport and Runner's World Italia), read in full: the classifications they report match. At the time of writing the official classification had not yet been published on the FIDAL website or on the timing pages we checked; should any discrepancies with the official document emerge, this article will be updated. The figures of 20,000 participants and 125 nations come from the Runner's World report. We were not at the race. Update of 23 November: the official classification (TDS timing, published on the FIDAL website) is now available and confirms the positions and times reported; we have added it to the sources and, from it, filled in the men's fourth and fifth places. No discrepancies emerged.

Sources

  2. Runner's World Italia, Milano Half Marathon: la pioggia non ferma i 20mila runner. I risultati, 22 November 2026: podiums with absolute times, Chiappinelli's comeback from injury, Hassan Nour's debut, 10K results, context (weather, participants, nations).
  3. FIDAL, Powerade Milano Half Marathon — official general classification (TDS timing), race of 22 November 2026: positions, official times and gaps, consulted on 23 November 2026.
