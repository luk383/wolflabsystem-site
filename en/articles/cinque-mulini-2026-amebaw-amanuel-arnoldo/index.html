Cinque Mulini 2026: Amebaw and Amanuel win, Lucia Arnoldo takes third

At a glance

  • Likina Amebaw wins the women's race at the 94th Cinque Mulini in 19:54 over 6.2 km, ahead of Cynthia Chepkirui and Lucia Arnoldo.12
  • Saymon Amanuel retains the men's title in 23:28 over 8.2 km. Luca Alfieri finishes sixth in 24:23 and Mauro Dallapiccola eighth in 24:37.12
  • The World Athletics Cross Country Tour Gold meeting took place in San Vittore Olona on Sunday 22 November. These are cross-country results: terrain and course distances prevent direct comparisons with road times.

Amebaw clear, Arnoldo reaches the podium

Likina Amebaw takes a clear victory in the women's race. The Ethiopian finishes in 19:54, 46 seconds ahead of Kenya's Cynthia Chepkirui in 20:40. FIDAL and World Athletics agree on the result and the growing lead, but their intermediate gaps differ by one second. We report only the final margins here.12

Lucia Arnoldo takes third in 20:58, on her debut for Fiamme Oro according to FIDAL. She moves past Elvanie Nimbona on the final lap; Nimbona finishes sixth in 21:19, behind Federica Zanne in fourth with 21:03 and Micol Majori in fifth with 21:04. Both federations describe this as Amebaw's third Gold-level cross-country victory of the season after Amorebieta and Seville.12

Amanuel repeats, Alfieri leads the Italians

Saymon Amanuel wins again after his 2025 success. He opens a gap on the second of four laps and extends it to finish in 23:28. Célestin Ndikumana takes second in 23:57 and Frankline Kibet third in 24:05.12

Luca Alfieri misses the top five by one second: Germany's Markus Görger finishes in 24:22 and the Italian in 24:23. Mauro Dallapiccola is eighth in 24:37 and Abderrazzak Gasmi ninth in 24:56. These are overall placings reported by both sources, not a national-team selection ranking.12

Short course and under-20 races

FIDAL reports short-course wins for Sebastiano Parolini in 10:00 and Valeria Minati in 11:36. Vittore Borromini wins the men's under-20 race in 12:35, while Mimosa Miari Fulcis takes the women's in 14:43. These are separate races from the international events listed above.1

Limitations

This article reconstructs the 22 November results from FIDAL and World Athletics federation reports, read on 23 November. The tables show the first six finishers, not the full classification or an independent check of the timing system. We did not attend the race or reproduce organiser photographs. Inconsistent intermediate gaps are omitted; the final times and placings reported here agree across both sources.
