Method

Wolf Lab is a lab on endurance training: running, cycling, nutrition and strength for people who train as amateurs.

Where we start

First systematic reviews and guidelines from scientific bodies, then single studies, then the experience of coaches and scientists who explain their reasoning. Forums and social media help us understand the questions, not provide the answers.

The evidence level

Strong evidence Several reviews or guidelines agree.

Moderate evidence Some good-quality studies, or solid evidence for the principle but not for the practical application.

Limited evidence Few or small studies, or mainly coaching experience.

Nothing to sell you

The site has no ads, sponsors or affiliate links. When we mention a product it's because the example needs it, not because someone pays.

Artificial intelligence

We use AI as an assistant: to find studies, summarise them and prepare a first draft. Every article is then reviewed and the key figures are checked against the original sources.

Not medical advice

The information is general. For pain, injuries, medical conditions or specific diets, talk to a doctor or registered dietitian.

Running, cycling, nutrition and training for amateurs, grounded in evidence. This information doesn't replace advice from a doctor.
