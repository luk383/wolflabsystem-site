At a glance

  • On 25 November Nike announced the first Gyakusou collection in more than five years, made with Jun Takahashi: cold-weather running apparel and two special-edition shoes, the Nike Alphafly 4 SP and Nike Vomero Plus SP.1
  • Debut on 3 December at Undercover stores, undercoverism.com, Dover Street Market London and the Shanghai Marathon Expo; global launch on 11 December on nike.com, the SNKRS app and selected retailers.1
  • Prices have not been announced: neither Nike's release nor the trade press we read gives them.12

What Gyakusou is

Gyakusou means "running in reverse" in Japanese. According to Nike the line has existed for 16 years and combines Nike innovation with the vision of Jun Takahashi, designer and runner. Business of Fashion notes the project began in 2010 and that Takahashi worked on models such as the Zoom Fly SP, Pegasus 36 Trail and ZoomX Vaporfly Next%, before the five-year break.12

What the collection includes

The apparel is designed for cold days: lightweight down jackets and vests with Nike AeroLoft bonded insulation and water-repellent materials, performance running tights and pants, T-shirts and GIRA gloves. Nike speaks of warmth, protection, breathability and freedom of movement. On colour, the collection goes for tonal whites and natural greens.1

There are two shoes. According to Nike, the Alphafly 4 SP helps runners hold speed over the marathon distance, while the Vomero Plus SP offers maximum cushioning for everyday runs.1

Dates and outlets

On 3 December the collection debuts at Undercover stores, on undercoverism.com and at Dover Street Market London, as well as at the Shanghai Marathon Expo and in Nike Running and NikeLab stores in Shanghai. The global launch follows on 11 December on nike.com, the SNKRS app and selected retailers.13 The release does not say whether or when the pieces will reach Italy, or in which stores.

What we do not know

In the material we read there are no prices, quantities, sizes or shoe specifications (weight, drop, foam, plate), and no date for Italy. Business of Fashion states explicitly that prices have not been revealed yet.2 Until they are, we cannot say how these shoes sit against the standard Alphafly and Vomero models.

Limits

We rely on Nike's official 25 November release and two trade outlets, and we have neither tested nor seen the garments or shoes in person. Functional descriptions such as warmth, protection and cushioning are Nike's statements, not test results. We do not cover resale prices or offers.
