Price and availability in Italy

On 19 November HONOR brought the Watch 6 Pro to international markets, two months after its Chinese launch. We checked the official Italian store directly: the black version with the fluoroelastomer strap is listed at €379.90 (or 3 interest-free instalments of €126.63), with two discounts shown on the page: a €100 coupon and an offer with code AAW6P100 bringing the final price to €279.90. The cart includes at no cost a brown leather strap, HONOR CHOICE Earbuds Clip earphones and the 12-month HONOR Care Pro package (declared value €139.60).1

According to Android Authority, citing the European price list, euro pricing applies to France, Germany, Italy and Spain: €379 for the black version and €429 for the silver one with the steel strap, with promotional prices of €279 and €329 tied to vouchers and launch offers. At the time of our check on the Italian store, the silver steel-strap version was not listed on the page: we treat it as an announced price, not one confirmed for Italy.13

For runners

The official product page leads with "Pro Running Metrics": a Running Coach with lactate-threshold heart-rate monitoring for targeted sessions, and Route Back navigation to retrace your outward route. The positioning system is described as a dual-band three-antenna setup ("Dual-Band Three-Antenna Setup"), designed to hold a satellite fix regardless of wrist angle. These are manufacturer claims: there are no independent tests yet measuring GPS accuracy or the threshold estimate.2

The rest of the spec sheet, as reported by Android Authority: a 46.3 mm case at 50 g without the strap, stainless steel frame with a nanocrystalline ceramic bezel, over 120 sports modes, 5 ATM water resistance and IP68/IP68K/IP69 ratings, Bluetooth 5.4, NFC, Android and iOS compatibility.3

Display and battery: the claimed numbers

The display is a 1.5-inch dual-layer ("Tandem") OLED at 466×466 resolution; HONOR claims a 5,500-nit peak, which the spec sheet notes is reached with only part of the screen lit. The 515 mAh battery comes with three estimates backed by explicit usage conditions on the official page: up to 18 days in ultra-long mode (smart heart-rate tracking, overnight sleep monitoring, 90 minutes of weekly GPS workouts), up to 10 days in typical use (continuous heart rate, SpO2 and stress tracking) and up to 7 days with the always-on display. These are manufacturer-defined scenarios: real-world battery life depends on usage, and for frequent GPS runners it will sit closer to the low end.23

The feature that cannot be advertised

At the Chinese launch HONOR presented cardiac arrest detection among its cardiovascular features; it is absent from the European promotional material. Asked by Android Authority, the company clarified that the feature is supported on the European version but cannot be promoted because EU medical certification is still in progress: until it is completed, HONOR is not permitted to market it in Europe. In practice: the feature exists, but it is not validated as a medical device and should be treated accordingly — no consumer feature replaces a medical evaluation.3

The context

It is the second Chinese launch of the week after the Xiaomi Watch S5 and Smart Band 11, but at a different tier: €379.90 against Xiaomi's €129-199, with genuine sportwatch ambitions (dual-band GPS, running metrics) that put it closer in price to the €499 COROS Dura 2 Solar than to generalist smartwatches. The difference remains the sports ecosystem and independent validation, which HONOR has yet to build.

Limitations

This article is based on the official Italian store, HONOR's global product page and Android Authority's coverage: no WOLFLAB test and no independent test available at publication time. Battery life, brightness, GPS accuracy and Running Coach features are manufacturer claims. Promotional prices depend on codes and vouchers whose availability can change; the silver/steel version's price (€429) is announced for euro markets but was not listed on the Italian store at verification time. Cardiac arrest detection is not a certified medical device function in the EU.
