In brief

  • Lorenzo Lotti (ASD Sempre di Corsa) won the San Francesco Marathon 2026 in 2:32:19, his third victory after 2023 and 2025. Andrea Patrian (Atletica Trento) finished second 46 seconds back, with Francesco Bernardi third in 2:48:10.12
  • The women's race went to Liza Bellandi (G.S. Orecchiella Garfagnana) in 3:25:51, ahead of Concetta Della Rocca (3:31:15) and Cecilia Enuca (3:36:37).12
  • A record edition: over 2,300 entries, the half marathon and 10K sold out, athletes from 39 countries and 425 clubs on the start line.12
  • In the half marathon Marco Cecchetti (1:12:10) and Giulia Giorgi (1:17:36) took the wins; in the 10K Giacomo Castellini (32:56) and Silvia Tamburi (36:38).12

Lotti drops Patrian by 46 seconds

The third edition of the San Francesco Marathon was held on Sunday 22 November in Santa Maria degli Angeli, in the municipality of Assisi, starting at 9:15. Lorenzo Lotti, already the winner in 2023 and 2025, covered the 42 kilometres in 2:32:19, dropping Andrea Patrian (Atletica Trento) by 46 seconds; Patrian finished in 2:33:05. Third place went to Francesco Bernardi (Atletica MDS Panariagroup) in 2:48:10.12

The women's race went to Liza Bellandi (G.S. Orecchiella Garfagnana), winner in 3:25:51. Behind her came Concetta Della Rocca, second in 3:31:15, and Cecilia Enuca (Ternana Marathon Club), third in 3:36:37.12

Cecchetti and Giorgi in the half, Castellini and Tamburi in the 10K

In the half marathon, 17 seconds over Alessandro Mezzetti (Atletica Capanne) were enough for Marco Cecchetti (Atletica Avis Sarnano) to win in 1:12:10; American Michael Scott was third in 1:12:57. In the women's race Giulia Giorgi (Calcestruzzi Corradini Excelsior) finished in 1:17:36, ahead of Giulia Cappelli (ASD Go Running) and Cristina Mercuri (Atletica Winner Foligno).12

In the competitive 10 kilometres Giacomo Castellini (Atletica Perugia Team) won in 32:56, nine seconds ahead of Alessio Malfagia; third was Simone Trombettoni, Malfagia's teammate at Podistica Lino Spagnoli. The women's race went to Silvia Tamburi (Atletica Avis Perugia) in 36:38, ahead of Cassandra Ulivieri and Marzia Stella Yousif (ASD Atletica Taino).12

A record edition, with inclusion at its centre

The event passed 2,300 entries for the first time, a new record, with the half marathon and 10K sold out. Sixty-eight percent of participants came from outside Umbria, every Italian region was represented, and foreign athletes came from 39 countries across four continents; 425 clubs were on the start line and women made up 31% of entries. The 2026 edition took place in the year of celebrations for the eighth centenary of the Transit of Saint Francis.12

The organisers did not use free bibs to sign elite runners: the only ones went to athletes with disabilities and their guides. The joëlette crews started five minutes before the gun. The weekend had opened on Saturday with the Wolfy Dog Run, the dog walk, and the "Vieni con me" walk to discover the city.12

Limitations

This is a results piece based on two independent local journalistic reports (Assisi Oggi and Assisi Sport), read in full: every time and position they report matches. At the time of writing we could not find the full official classification online (the race's FIDAL page carries no link to results); should the official document show discrepancies, this article will be updated. Entry and origin figures come from the two reports, which cite the organisers' numbers. We were not at the race.
