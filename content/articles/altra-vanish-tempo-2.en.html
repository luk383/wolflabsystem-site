What the Vanish Tempo 2 is

Altra positions it as the shoe for quality workouts and faster sessions, alongside the Vanish Carbon 3 built for race day. It shares the overall layout with its bigger sibling - a 4 mm drop instead of the brand's traditional zero drop, supercritical foam, a split plate - but with different recipes: the foam here is FAST TRN (the Carbon 3 uses FAST RCE) and the plate is PEBAX, a technical polymer, not carbon.2,3

According to Altra, the split PEBAX plate is designed to deliver propulsion without compromising natural forefoot movement, and the rocker geometry supports the toe-off. The upper is a breathable engineered mesh with a padded heel and soft tongue, more substantial than the Carbon 3's; an independent lower eyelet lets you tighten without constriction.1

Fit and sizing

The Vanish Tempo 2 uses FootShape Racing, the snuggest of Altra's three fits: toe room in the forefoot, but a more wrapped midfoot than the Standard and Original versions. It should not be read as "the usual wide Altra", even though the product page notes that every Altra keeps room for the toes. Explicit warning from the maker: the shoe runs small, and they suggest ordering half a size up.1

What the first test says

Slowtwitch's Ryan Heisler, who had already reviewed the Carbon 3 very positively, describes the Tempo 2 as a surprise: more structured and stable than the racer, with a wide platform underfoot, and in his testing on varied routes "as fast as nearly all the carbon racers" he has tried, with a perceived advantage uphill thanks to the split plate's flexibility. His technical takeaway: it is not the plate alone that makes speed, but the combination of foam and geometry.2

These are one tester's conclusions, with no lab data or running-economy measurements: we report them as a reasoned opinion, not an objective finding. WOLFLAB has not tested the shoe.2

Price and availability

On Altra's Italian site the men's Vanish Tempo 2 is listed at 200 euros in three colourways; endurance.biz reports a UK price of 175 pounds and availability through Altra's direct e-commerce and selected specialty running retailers. The US-based tester cites 200 dollars against the Carbon 3's 240. We have not verified availability, sizes or shipping for Italy beyond the product page.1,2,3

Limits

  • Weights and measurements are maker-declared and vary by size: 243 g men and 208 g women per endurance.biz, 243 g (8.5 oz) on the Italian page.1,3
  • The speed and stability judgement comes from a single journalistic test: no instrumented data, no measured comparison against other shoes.2
  • No WOLFLAB test and no independent verification of Altra's claims (energy return, propulsion).
  • Actual availability in Italian shops and shipping times not verified.
