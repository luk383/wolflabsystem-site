In brief

  • The Suunto Race 3S launched on 15 October 2026: a 46 mm titanium case at 56 grams, up to 38 hours of dual-band GPS, a 2,000-nit AMOLED display. Price: €499 in Italy, $599 in the United States.
  • Unlike many launches, independent tests already exist: GPS improved over the predecessor, but a touchscreen that misfires with sweat and rain and third-party integrations that are still unstable.
  • The AI Coach is a paid conversational service in the Suunto app: three months free for buyers, the price after the trial not yet announced. It is a debut for Suunto, not a category first.

What Suunto claims

The Race 3S is the compact version of Suunto's trail flagship, announced on 15 October 2026 on the official blog (some trade articles date it 2 November: that is their publication date, not the launch). The declared specifications:12

What independent tests say

The most in-depth review read in full (RunToTheFinish, 1 November, after weeks of use on trails, roads and in the gym) gives a strongly positive verdict and reports:3

  • GPS accuracy improved over the Suunto Run 2 on its test routes.
  • Turn-by-turn navigation with turn alerts and climb and descent notifications that work.
  • Cons: the touchscreen swipes to a new screen when sweat or rain slides across the glass mid-run; third-party app integration is still "buggy"; offline maps take a long time to download; the price rose by $150 over the predecessor.

Other reviews published between October and November (Runnea, RunMorph, ALS Sport) confirm the device has been tested by several outlets: this is not a paper-only launch.3

AI Coach: what it is and what it costs

With the Race 3S, Suunto AI Coach debuts: a conversational assistant in the Suunto app that answers questions about your data with text, charts and maps, and can create structured workouts, plans and routes to sync to the watch. It is Suunto's first AI product, in a category where automated coaching features already exist (Garmin Coach, Amazfit's Zepp Coach): not an absolute first.1

The economic point matters most: AI Coach is a paid service. Buyers who purchase and pair a Race 3S by activating it in the app by 31 December 2026 get three months free; the subscription price after that has not been announced yet. Judging the watch while counting on the AI Coach therefore means buying without knowing the full cost of one of its headline features.4

Price and availability

Verified on 6 November 2026: €499 at Italian online retailers and according to the specialist press, $599 on Suunto's American store. The Race 3S is already on sale, not on pre-order.35

What is only claimed

  • The 45 hours in Endurance mode and 38 GPS hours are Suunto lab figures, with the manufacturer's own caveat that real battery life varies with settings and conditions.
  • The accuracy of the new optical heart-rate module is claimed: no independent validation specific to this sensor was read.
  • The "+233%" brightness is Suunto's comparison with its own Race S, not a third-party measurement.

For a watch with another ecosystem's recovery metrics at a much lower price, see Motorola moto watch 2 fit with Polar metrics; for reviewed running GPS watches, the GPS watches guide.

Limits

  • Only one in-depth independent review read in full (RunToTheFinish); the others cited exist but were not read end to end.
  • No long-term data on touchscreen reliability and integrations: the reported problems may be fixed by firmware updates.
  • Prices verified on 6 November 2026: they can change; the AI Coach cost after the three free months remains unknown at publication time.
