In short

  • On 8 November 2026 Manazot Siyum Taferi (Ethiopia) won the 43rd Athens Marathon. The Authentic in 2:10:03, a new record for the Marathon-to-Athens course.
  • In the women's race Yatane Dusa (Ethiopia) won in 2:30:04, also a course record.
  • The figures come from concordant news reports; full official results had not been published at verification time. We do not analyse splits or shoes: the data is not in the sources.

Men's marathon

Taferi finished in 2:10:03, taking 31 seconds off the course record of 2:10:34 set by Kenya's Edwin Kiptoo in 2023. The finish is in Athens' Panathenaic Stadium, at the end of the original route from the town of Marathon.1,2

  1. Manazot Siyum Taferi (ETH): 2:10:03, course record
  2. Assefa Tefera Mengisa (ETH): 2:10:18
  3. Patrick Keter Kiplagat (KEN): 2:11:22

Vincent Kipsang Rono (KEN) was fourth in 2:12:06.1

Women's marathon

Dusa won in 2:30:04, lowering by more than a minute the course record of 2:31:06 set by Lithuania's Rasa Drazdauskaite, which had stood since 2010.2

  1. Yatane Dusa (ETH): 2:30:04, course record
  2. Tsiyon Temesgen (ETH): 2:30:29
  3. Jane Chemkemboy Bareikis (USA): 2:32:59

Context

The Athens running event ran over two days: organisers and local media report more than 85,000 participants from 124 countries across the various distances, a figure carried by the sources and not independently verifiable.1 The marathon itself was run on Sunday 8 November 2026, the 43rd edition of the race on the route of the Philippides legend.
