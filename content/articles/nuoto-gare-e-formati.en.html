In brief

  • These are four different things: open water swimming (a sport on its own), triathlon (a multisport with a swim), IRONMAN (a brand of long-distance triathlon races) and HYROX (running plus strength stations, no swimming).
  • The distances below come from the organisers' official sites. We publish no calendars, dates or entries: they change every season.
  • HYROX and IRONMAN are brands of private organisers. Wolf Lab is not affiliated with any of the bodies cited.

Open water swimming

World Aquatics defines open water as any competition in rivers, lakes, oceans or water channels. The Olympic programme, in the regulations in force from June 2025, is the men's and women's 10 km.1 The World Championships programme and other distances change by edition: check the current regulations and the body's site.

Triathlon (World Triathlon)

According to World Triathlon, the Standard (Olympic) format has a 1.5 km swim; Sprint is half of it: 750 m swim, 20 km bike and 5 km run.2 In triathlon the swim is the first leg, before the bike and the run.

IRONMAN: multisport context

IRONMAN is a race series run by a company. By the organiser's definition an IRONMAN is a 3.8 km swim, 180 km bike and 42.2 km run on the same day, in that order (226 km in total). IRONMAN 70.3 is half: 1.9 km swim, 90 km bike and 21.1 km run.3 Typical finish times given by the organiser are 5-8 hours for a 70.3 and 10-17 hours for an IRONMAN.3

HYROX: a separate discipline, no swimming

HYROX is an indoor fitness competition: a 1 km run followed by one station, repeated 8 times. The stations, in order, are 1000 m SkiErg, 50 m sled push, 50 m sled pull, 80 m burpee broad jump, 1000 m row, 200 m farmers carry, 100 m sandbag lunges and 100 wall balls. The format is the same worldwide; weights change by category.45 It is not swimming and we do not treat it as such: it sits here only because the site covers endurance and hybrid racing.

What we do not do

  • We publish no calendars, dates or prices for these races: we have not verified them. Use the official sites.
  • We do not say how long they take you: the organiser's times are indicative and cover all participants.
  • We offer no training plans for these formats: for HYROX and IRONMAN we do not yet have enough sources.

What we know and what we do not

Distances and format are as stated by the bodies and organisers. We have not verified the full rules of every category (for HYROX we do not report weights) or the rules of each event.
