In brief

  • These are four different things: open water swimming (a sport on its own), triathlon (a multisport with a swim), IRONMAN (a brand of long-distance triathlon races) and HYROX (running plus strength stations, no swimming).
  • The distances below come from the organisers' official sites. We do not copy calendars, dates or entries: they change every season. Official links are at the bottom of the page.
  • HYROX and IRONMAN are brands of private organisers. Wolf Lab is not affiliated with any of the bodies cited.

Open water swimming

World Aquatics defines open water as any competition in rivers, lakes, oceans or water channels. The Olympic programme, in the June 2025 regulations (later version: February 2026), is the men's and women's 10 km.1 For the World Championships programme and other distances, the World Aquatics regulations in force (Part Three, section 1) and the body's site apply.

Triathlon (World Triathlon)

According to World Triathlon, the Standard (Olympic) format has a 1.5 km swim; Sprint is half of it: 750 m swim, 20 km bike and 5 km run.2 In triathlon the swim is the first leg, before the bike and the run.

IRONMAN: multisport context

IRONMAN is a race series run by a company. By the organiser's definition an IRONMAN is a 3.8 km swim, 180 km bike and 42.2 km run on the same day, in that order (226 km in total). IRONMAN 70.3 is half: 1.9 km swim, 90 km bike and 21.1 km run.3 Typical finish times given by the organiser are 5-8 hours for a 70.3 and 10-17 hours for an IRONMAN.3

On categories, the 2026 IRONMAN rules say age-group athletes compete in the division matching their age on 31 December of the event year.7 For World Championship qualification, the "Kona Standard" is not a cut-off time but a global benchmark used to compare performance across age groups and sexes.6 Cut-off times are published by each event: for example, the IRONMAN Wales athlete guide of 13 September 2026 gives a 2 hour 20 minute swim cut-off.8 Always check the guide of the event you enter.

HYROX: a separate discipline, no swimming

HYROX is an indoor fitness competition: a 1 km run followed by one station, repeated 8 times. The stations, in order, are 1000 m SkiErg, 50 m sled push, 50 m sled pull, 80 m burpee broad jump, 1000 m row, 200 m farmers carry, 100 m sandbag lunges and 100 wall balls. The format is the same worldwide; weights change by category.45 It is not swimming and we do not treat it as such: it sits here only because the site covers endurance and hybrid racing.

In the 26/27 Singles rulebook there are four divisions (Women, Women Pro, Men, Men Pro): weights and gender change, not distances. Weights from the 26/27 season Singles rulebook (sled weights include the sled): sled push 102 kg (Women), 152 kg (Men and Women Pro), 202 kg (Men Pro); sled pull 78, 103, 153 kg; farmers carry 2 × 16, 24, 32 kg; sandbag lunges 10, 20, 30 kg; wall balls 4, 6, 9 kg.5 Doubles, Team Relay and Adaptive formats also exist, each with its own rules.9

Calendars: where to find them

Each body publishes its own calendar and it changes every season: here are links to the official pages, not a copy.

  • Open water in Italy: the FIN Open Water Grand Prix page lists the season's races with date, distance and location.10
  • IRONMAN and IRONMAN 70.3: the official Race Finder, filterable by race type and area.11
  • HYROX: the official Find My Race page with upcoming races.12

For a triathlon under World Triathlon rules also check your country's federation website.

What we do not do

  • We do not copy calendars, dates or prices: they change every season and each body publishes its own. Official links are in the previous section.
  • We do not say how long they take you: the organiser's times are indicative and cover all participants.
  • We offer no training plans for these formats: for HYROX and IRONMAN we do not yet have enough sources on how to prepare.

What we know and what we do not

Distances and formats are as stated by the bodies and organisers. For penalties, category rules, each event's cut-offs and the programme of a single edition, the official regulations in force apply.
