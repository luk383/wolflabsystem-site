Turin: entries at €100 until 20 November

The race's official page, checked today, 18 November, shows entries "still open" for the 2026 Torino City Marathon at €100 until 20 November. Entries are completed on the ENDU platform, linked from the official site.123

At the event presentation, FIDAL reported almost 8,000 entries across the competitive races, with a stated target of 10,000 participants overall. In the marathon, nearly 35% of athletes come from abroad.3

The race weekend. On Saturday 28 November, the Torino City Run: 5 non-competitive kilometres to run or walk, entirely inside Parco del Valentino; €5 of each entry goes to the Fondazione Allegra Agnelli. On Sunday 29 November, the marathon (42.195 km) and half marathon start together at 8:30 from Piazza Vittorio Veneto: the two courses share the first 20 km, then split. The marathon finishes in Piazza Castello, the half at the I Viali shopping centre in Nichelino, with shuttles for athletes. The marathon course is FIDAL-certified, mostly flat, and crosses Turin, Moncalieri, Beinasco and Nichelino.31

The Torino City Marathon Green Pea Village is at Via Ernesto Fenoglietti 20, in the Lingotto district, open Friday 27 and Saturday 28 November from 10:00 to 20:00. The visit is mandatory for every entrant: bib and race pack are collected only there, with no Sunday-morning collection.3

Florence: the marathon is sold out, the 10K stays open until 19 November

The 42nd Estra Firenze Marathon is sold out: the organiser announced it on 27 August, three months before the start, with 13,000 entrants, around 50% from abroad and 27% women. This is not today's news: we flag it because race weekend is eleven days away and the remaining options have close deadlines.47

The marathon waiting list ran from 3 to 30 September and is now closed. According to the organiser, one route into the marathon remains: the official Destination Florence packages, which bundle bib, hotel and services; availability and prices must be checked on the official site, as the sources we read report no remaining quotas.64

The competitive 10K Firenze Marathon runs on Sunday 29 November, starting at 8:45 from Via Tornabuoni on a FIDAL-certified 10 km course. Bibs are capped at 3,000 and entries are shown as open until Thursday 19 November, unless the cap is reached earlier, on the ENDU platform. The 10K entry fee does not appear on the official pages we read: check it at registration.54

On Saturday 28 November, the programme opens with the San Benedetto Ginky Family Run (about 3 km for children and families, from the Ippodromo del Visarno) and the 3x7K Fiorentina Charity Run (10:30 start, 3-hour time limit, supporting the Charity Program organisations).4

Limits

Entry status is a snapshot from this morning, 18 November: deadlines can close early if bibs run out, and conditions can change without notice. For Turin, the sources we read state neither the fee after 20 November nor whether entries continue beyond that date. The figure of almost 8,000 entries in Turin was reported by FIDAL at the presentation and carried by local press; it is not a live count. We did not verify remaining availability on the ENDU platform in real time: check the actual status on the entry page before paying.
