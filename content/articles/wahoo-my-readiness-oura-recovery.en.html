In brief

  • On 12 November 2026 Wahoo and Oura announced the integration of Oura data (heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep) into My Readiness, the Wahoo App feature that gauges how ready you are to train.1
  • The novelty is the Recovery Response score: it reads recovery signals in the context of your training load instead of on their own.12
  • My Readiness is free for all Wahoo App users, but it requires an active Oura membership (5.99 euros a month or 69.99 euros a year in the EU, per Oura's official support). At launch Oura is the only supported data source.14
  • Part of the history and the adaptive workout recommendations stay behind Wahoo's paid CORE and PRO plans.23

What it adds: recovery read in the context of training

Until now recovery data (ring, watch) and training data (platform) lived in separate places. My Readiness joins them: the new Recovery Response score, on a -100 to +100 scale, tells you whether your body is recovering as expected after the load you have done, better or worse.2

The manufacturer's example: after a hard VO2max interval session, a drop in heart rate variability and a higher resting heart rate are the expected cost of that session, and the app still suggests an endurance ride. The very same dip after an easy week, with no training to explain it, is flagged as an anomaly with advice to hold back.1

Recovery Response works together with two other scores: Training Capacity (the balance between short-term and long-term load, calculated from your Wahoo history) and Mental Capacity (a 90-second reaction test plus a daily check-in). Together they produce one of six readiness states: Primed, Ready, Under Stress, Depleted, Rest Recommended and Anomalies Detected.2

Requirements: Oura only, and at least 7 days of data

At launch the only recovery data source you can connect is Oura: all ring generations are supported, other integrations are announced but not available. To see Recovery Response and the readiness states you need at least 7 days of recovery data; when you connect, the app automatically imports up to 90 days of Oura history to calibrate your baseline.12

The feature is available in the Wahoo App, in SYSTM and in the Wahoo App for Apple Watch. Training Capacity requires an athlete profile (4DP for cycling, 3DP for running) and recorded activities; Mental Capacity requires the daily test.2

What is free and what stays paid

Wahoo states My Readiness is free for all Wahoo App users: readiness state and Recovery Response, with their history, are visible to everyone. Staying behind the paid plans are Training Capacity and Mental Capacity history (CORE and PRO only) and, according to the plans page, the adaptive workout recommendations (PRO tier).123

The real cost, though, runs through Oura: you need an active Oura membership, which Oura's official support lists at 5.99 euros a month or 69.99 euros a year in the EU, on top of the ring's price. Wahoo plans, if you need them, start at 4.99 US dollars a month (CORE) or 17.99 (PRO), plus taxes; there is a 14-day trial.43

Limits

  • Scores, thresholds and states are the manufacturer's conventions: there is no independent validation of Recovery Response and it is not a diagnostic tool. The "Anomalies Detected" state flags values outside your normal range, not an illness.
  • The free/paid split comes from Wahoo documentation read on 13 November 2026 and can change; the arrival of wearables beyond Oura is an announced plan, not a date.
  • Oura and Wahoo prices are read on 13 November 2026 and can change; taxes depend on the billing country.
