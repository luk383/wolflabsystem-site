At a glance

  • On 26 November 2026 ASICS announced the GEL-CUMULUS 29: FF LUXE foam, 2 mm more midsole thickness than the previous version, unchanged 8 mm drop, PureGEL technology under the heel and a full rubber outsole (AHAR LO and AHARPLUS).1
  • List price 150 euros, worldwide availability from 1 January 2027.1
  • The release states a carbon footprint of 12.0 kg CO₂ equivalent per pair. Weight and the full stack figures are not given on the page we read, and we have not tested the shoe.1

What changes

According to ASICS the shoe relies on FF LUXE foam, reinforced cushioning and a full rubber outsole, and aims to be light and versatile for everyday running. The extra 2 mm of midsole, compared with the previous version, reinforces cushioning underfoot, while the drop stays at 8 mm. PureGEL sits under the heel to absorb impact.1

The outsole is fully rubber, with AHAR LO and AHARPLUS technologies, which the release says give reliable grip and long life. The upper is a technical mesh with a padded tongue and an OrthoLite insole.1

Price and availability

The release gives 150 euros as the list price and 1 January 2027 as the worldwide availability date. It names no sales channels, colours or different dates for individual markets, so we do not report any.1

What ASICS says

Chris Brown, ASICS global product head for Performance Running, says that for the 29th generation the aim was to build on strengths runners already like, with "an even more comfortable experience" and the line's versatile DNA. These are company statements, not independent assessments. ASICS marathoner Koen Naert describes it as a shoe he keeps returning to for high mileage: that is a testimonial from a sponsored athlete.1

Limits

We read the official ASICS release in full (French version); we have not tried the shoe and have seen no lab measurements. The release gives no weight, absolute stack or comparison with other models, so we do not say whether it is lighter, softer or more durable than other shoes. The carbon footprint is a company-declared figure and we have not checked how it was calculated.
