The 12.50-euro kit: who it makes sense for

The Cog & Click Upgrade Kit is the steepest discount in percentage terms (-75%): it brings 24-gear virtual shifting and Zwift controls (shifting, steering, menus, Ride Ons) to the handlebars of any bike mounted on a compatible trainer. The official product page states it works with "a growing list of Zwift Cog Compatible smart trainers", installs without tools, and asks you to select your trainer brand and model from a menu: Elite, Garmin Tacx, JetBlack, Van Rysel, Wahoo and Zwift, plus an "Other" entry. The Click works with both Zwift and Rouvy.2

Watch the compatibility: it is not universal. Before buying, select your trainer from the dropdown on the product page, because the kit comes in different versions by brand and model.2

Before you buy

Three useful checks. First: the kit's compatibility with your trainer, as described above. Second: shipping costs and availability for your country are confirmed at checkout, not on the product page. Third: the discount covers hardware; the Zwift subscription remains a separate cost and was not on promotion on the shop at the time of verification.1
