In brief

  • The 31st Palermo Marathon runs on Sunday 15 November 2026, starting at 8:30 from viale della Libertà. A half marathon (21.097 km) and a 4×10.5 km relay complete the programme.1
  • The organiser expects about 3,300 runners from 67 nations: around 1,000 in the marathon, just over 2,000 in the half and 224 relay teams.1
  • Bibs are collected at the Expo in piazza Castelnuovo on Friday 13 and Saturday 14 November, 10:00-19:30. The Expo also opens on Sunday (6:00-14:30), but bib collection is stated for Friday and Saturday only.1
  • Pacers cover five bands: 3h10, 3h30, 3h45, 4h00 and 4h15. The organiser announces a Rai Sport highlights broadcast on Monday 16 at 18:45, not verified against the TV schedule.1

Before the start: bibs and Expo

Race bibs are collected at the Expo in piazza Castelnuovo (the pedestrian area by the Palchetto della Musica) on Friday 13 and Saturday 14 November, from 10:00 to 19:30.1

One detail that matters: the Expo also opens on Sunday 15, from 6:00 to 14:30, but the press release lists bib collection for Friday and Saturday only. If you plan to pick up your bib on race morning, do not take it for granted - check with the organisers first.1

Besides bib collection, the Expo hosts the presentation of the top runners, activities open to the public and a prevention point run by the Palermo health authority (Friday and Saturday, 10:00-16:00, including a free flu vaccination without booking).1

The three distances and the course

The marathon crosses the city twice over the same loop; the half marathon covers one loop. The 4×10.5 km relay shares the course, with three changeover zones: the first and third at Piazzale dei Matrimoni (viale Ercole 35, inside the Parco della Favorita), the second on viale della Libertà by the Giardino Inglese.1

The course starts on viale della Libertà, enters the Parco della Favorita, passes the Palazzina Cinese and heads back to the centre via via Dante and piazza Ruggero Settimo; then via Cavour, via Roma and the Cassaro (corso Vittorio Emanuele) up to Porta Nuova and through the grounds of the Royal Palace, returning along via Maqueda. It passes the Arab-Norman landmarks recognised by UNESCO: Teatro Massimo, Quattro Canti, the Cathedral and the Norman Palace.1

On race day: pacers and aid stations

Pacers cover five target bands: 3h10, 3h30, 3h45, 4h00 and 4h15, marked by coloured balloons.1

Aid stations and sponge points along the course are run by local running clubs: Equilibria Running Team, Marathon Monreale, Amatori Palermo and Park Run at the aid stations; Corleone Marathon, Park Run, Pegaso and Sicilia Running Team at the sponge points.1
