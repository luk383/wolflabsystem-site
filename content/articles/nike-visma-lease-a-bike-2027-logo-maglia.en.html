At a glance

  • On 25 November Team Visma | Lease a Bike announced it will expand its partnership with Nike Cycling in 2027: the Nike logo will appear on the race kit from next season.1
  • Nimbl keeps developing and making the kit and stays responsible for the shoes. Nike supports development with innovation and design expertise, and both brands' logos will be on the kit.12
  • No other teams have been announced: the team says it is the first to announce a Nike Cycling partnership for 2027.1

What was announced

Nike has supplied the Dutch team's riders and staff with off-bike apparel since the 2025 Tour de France. With the 25 November announcement the brand gains a visible presence in racing too: the Nike logo will be on the 2027 race kit alongside Nimbl's.1

According to the team's release, Nimbl continues to develop and manufacture the kit and to lead development of the cycling shoes, while Nike Cycling contributes its expertise in innovation and design. The stated aim is to develop and test new ideas together under the conditions in which the riders compete. CEO Richard Plugge speaks of "new possibilities" and of building something that moves the team and the sport forward.1

Where the logo will appear

The team told Cyclingnews the Nike logo will sit high on the front of the jersey and on the bib shorts. The shoes will not carry the logo as standard, but there will be more special-edition Nike shoes, like those Jonas Vingegaard wore at the 2026 Tour de France. Cyclingnews points out this does not mean Nike makes the kit or the shoes: that remains Nimbl's job.2

Where it starts from

The release recalls that the three partners already worked together at two Grand Tours in 2026: the team raced the final stage of the Giro d'Italia in the Nimbl x Nike Cycling Stardust kit, on the day Jonas Vingegaard won overall in Rome, and opened the Tour de France in the Nimbl x Nike Cycling Étoile shoe, winning the team time trial in Barcelona and taking yellow.1

What we do not know

The release gives no length for the deal, no financial terms, and no models or dates for Nike-branded fan apparel. On whether Nike will work with other teams, BikeRadar writes that Visma is the first to state a 2027 partnership and offers FDJ United-Suez as the editors' own hunch; it is not a confirmation from Nike or the team.3 Cyclingnews reports that Nike is already a lifestyle apparel partner of that team and of others.2

Limits

We rely on the team's release and two trade outlets. We have not seen the 2027 kit, which has not been presented yet, and we did not find a Nike press release on the same announcement. We do not cover prices or offers.
