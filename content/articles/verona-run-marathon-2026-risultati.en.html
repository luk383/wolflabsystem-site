In short

  • Abo Gelato (ETH) won the 25th Verona Run Marathon on 15 November 2026 in 2:12:03; Caroline Chebichi (KEN) won the women's race in 2:29:40, according to the final results published by World Athletics.1
  • Behind Gelato, three athletes within seven seconds: Lengen Lolkurraru 2:14:31, Vincent Kipsegechi Yator 2:14:32, Mathew Sang Kibiwot 2:14:38.1
  • First Italians: Luca Clara eighth in 2:20:34 and Mara Galvani fourth in 2:44:46.1

Men's marathon: Gelato clear, a tight pack behind

World Athletics has published the final marathon results: Abo Gelato, an Ethiopian born in 1994, finished in 2:12:03. The chasers arrived close together: Lengen Lolkurraru second in 2:14:31, Vincent Kipsegechi Yator third in 2:14:32, one second back, and Mathew Sang Kibiwot fourth in 2:14:38. The winner's margin over second place is 2 minutes 28 seconds. The results list finishing times, not the race dynamics, so we do not reconstruct how the gaps formed.1

Luca Clara was the first Italian, eighth overall in 2:20:34. Davide Angilella finished thirteenth in 2:24:38.1

Women's marathon: Chebichi in 2:29:40, Galvani fourth

Caroline Chebichi (KEN) won in 2:29:40 ahead of Addisalem Belay (ETH, 2:30:27) and Birkutan Abera (ETH, 2:33:58). Mara Galvani was fourth in 2:44:46, the first Italian, followed by Sofia Toniolo, fifth in 2:49:19.1
