In short

  • Langat won the 31st Palermo Marathon on 15 November 2026 in 2:14:46; Bertom won the women's race in 2:33:51, according to the organiser's race report.1
  • Vincenzo Agnello won the men's half marathon in 1:06:23 and Cristina Garcia Conde the women's in 1:24:40.1
  • The organiser reports just under 3,000 finishers across the event and 68 nations represented. This is not a count of marathon finishers alone.1

The men's marathon: Langat ahead of Tarus

The organiser describes Langat breaking away from Kelvin Kiptoo Tarus on an uphill section. Langat finished in 2:14:46, ahead of Tarus in 2:19:03 and Ishmael Chelanga Kalale in 2:20:15. All three are listed as Kenyan. This account comes from the race press release, not from our own observation or split-time analysis.1

Sebastiano Foti of Catania Running was seventh overall in 2:50:03, described by the organiser as the first Italian and first Sicilian finisher.1

The women's marathon: Bertom in 2:33:51

Bertom won in 2:33:51, ahead of Lenjiso in 2:38:37. Simona Pipitone of Marathon Athletic Avola completed the podium in 3:11:21 and is identified as the first Sicilian woman. The report spells the winner's first name in different ways and changes the order of the runner-up's names; we use their surnames here rather than silently choosing an unverified version.1

Half marathon: Agnello and Garcia Conde

Vincenzo Agnello of Sicilia Running Team won in 1:06:23, ahead of Tomas Michalec in 1:14:09 and Davide Di Maio in 1:15:08. Cristina Garcia Conde of Team Atletica Palermo won the women's race in 1:24:40, ahead of Sara Coppola in 1:33:36 and Emilia D'Anna in 1:37:06.1

Finishers are not entries

The post-race report gives just under 3,000 finishers and 68 nations represented for an event including the marathon, half marathon and relays. Do not read that as 3,000 marathon finishers, or compare it directly with the pre-race estimate of around 3,300 entries from 67 nations: entries and finishers are different measures, taken at different times. The organiser calls the participation a record for the event; we have not independently checked a historical series and do not present it as a verified federation record.1

Limits and related reading

This is a results report based on the organiser's 15 November release, not an independently checked certified results list. Gun versus net timing, full classifications and athlete identity records have not been verified.
