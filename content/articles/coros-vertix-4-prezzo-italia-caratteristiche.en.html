At a glance

  • COROS announced the VERTIX 4 on 24 November as its new GPS expedition watch: in Italy it goes on sale on Friday 27 November at a list price of 799 euros, in Black and Silver, with two straps in the box.23
  • 1.47-inch AMOLED display with sapphire glass and a 3,500-nit peak, an LED flashlight built into the right side, 64 GB for offline maps and music, and up to 105 hours of standard GPS battery life according to the manufacturer.1
  • In the first in-depth test published, DC Rainmaker records some wobbles in GPS and heart-rate accuracy and still-rough flashlight software, while praising the display, battery life and the two included straps.4

Key specifications

The VERTIX 4 succeeds the VERTIX 2S, released two and a half years ago, and inherits its role as the watch for mountaineering, trail running and winter expeditions. The 49.4 x 49.4 x 13.8 mm case pairs a titanium bezel, which doubles as the dual-frequency (L1 + L5) GPS antenna, with a fiber-reinforced composite core: the body weighs 60 grams, 84 with the silicone strap and 68 with the nylon one. The manufacturer claims a body 14% thinner than the VERTIX 2S, 10 ATM water resistance and an operating range from -30 to 50 degrees Celsius.1

The display is a 1.47-inch LTPO AMOLED (466 x 466 pixels) with sapphire glass and up to 3,500 nits. The integrated LED flashlight on the right side points where your hands are working, a choice COROS defended in its presentation and one already dividing opinion. Dual noise-cancelling microphones and a speaker add calls and voice control, and 64 GB of storage hold offline topographic and street maps plus music. Satellite maps, downloadable without a subscription, will reach the public in early 2027: they are not available at launch.14

Claimed battery life

Official figures promise up to 30 days of daily wear with health and sleep monitoring active, up to 105 hours in standard GPS mode and up to 60 hours in dual-frequency mode. The automatic satellite-switching mode found on the Pace 4 Pro is missing, though: the only options are forced multiband, all systems or GPS only, a gap also flagged by DC Rainmaker's test.14

What changes from the VERTIX 2S

From the first independent test's list: the display moves from MIP to AMOLED, storage doubles from 32 to 64 GB, quick-release straps go from 26 to 24 mm, thickness drops by 14%, the digital crown moves to the upper right to make room for the flashlight, a new Action button appears, the optical heart-rate sensor reaches its fourth generation, and offline maps gain street and trail names plus points of interest. In the United States the price rises from 699 to 799 dollars; in Italy the list price is 799 euros.24

The first independent test

DC Rainmaker, who tested the VERTIX 4 ahead of launch, praises the display, the build and the two-strap package, but flags some wobble in GPS and heart-rate accuracy, also seen by other reviewers, and flashlight software that still feels raw: no auto-off, no cadence-locked running mode like Garmin and Amazfit offer, and an awkward activation sequence mid-workout. The side-mounted flashlight proved useful for being seen by cars on an evening ride, less so for lighting the road at speed. He also notes that launching on Black Friday puts the VERTIX 4 head-to-head with the Garmin Fenix 8, which in his judgment offers a more versatile flashlight, more accurate GPS and heart rate, dive certification and offline music apps like Spotify, while COROS answers with longer battery life, a brighter display and the two included straps.4

Price and availability in Italy

The VERTIX 4 goes on sale in Italy on Friday 27 November at a list price of 799 euros, confirmed on COROS's official Italian store, in Black and Silver. The box includes both 24 mm quick-release straps, silicone and nylon, plus the USB-C charging adapter.23

Limitations

We have not tested the device: battery life, brightness and operating temperatures are manufacturer claims, and the first independent test available covers only a few days of use. Satellite maps are not available at launch: COROS announces them for early 2027. The price quoted is the official Italian list price; we do not cover deals or promotions.
