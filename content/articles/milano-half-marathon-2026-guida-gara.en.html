In short

  • On Sunday 22 November the Powerade Milano Half Marathon takes place: a competitive half marathon (21.097 km) and a 10 km, competitive and non-competitive. According to the Corriere della Sera it is a record sell-out with over 20,000 entrants, 5,000 more than in 2025.3
  • The start is at 8:45 from viale Eginardo (CityLife area, M5 Portello): the regulation marks the time as provisional. Time limit 2h45' for the 21 km, 90' for the 10 km.1
  • Bibs are collected only at the Expo Village inside the Allianz MiCo (gate 7 entrance): Friday 20 from 10:00 to 19:00 and Saturday 21 from 9:30 to 19:00. No bib collection on Sunday.14
  • A nearly flat course (about 40 m of total elevation gain) past the Castello Sforzesco, La Scala, San Babila and the Darsena; refreshment points around km 5, 10, 15 and 20, pacers on the half marathon only, live tracking with Trackanza and live music by Rockin'1000 along the course.23

A record sell-out

Milan's half marathon closes the season with its biggest numbers ever: according to the Corriere della Sera more than 20,000 will be at the start, five thousand more than the 2025 edition. According to MilanoToday over half of the entrants come from abroad; according to the Corriere more than half are under 35 and 13% are under 24. These are figures communicated at the event presentation and reported by the press, not an official count published by the organiser.34

The race weekend, from the official documents

Bib collection. Expo Village at the Allianz MiCo in viale Ludovico Scarampo, entrance from gate 7: Friday 20 November from 10:00 to 19:00 and Saturday 21 November from 9:30 to 19:00. The regulation is categorical: no race bib can be collected on Sunday.14

Sunday 22. The bag drop opens at 7:15 and closes at 8:30. The start is set for 8:45 from viale Eginardo, next to the Allianz MiCo, the event's logistical headquarters; the regulation marks it with an asterisk as a provisional time, so it is worth re-checking the official channels in the coming days. Time limits: 2 hours 45 minutes for the half marathon, 90 minutes for the 10 km; beyond these the organiser can no longer guarantee full course closure. Post-race services at the MiCo stay open until 13:00.14

The course: a crossing of the centre

An almost flat route, about 40 metres of total elevation gain, FIDAL-approved, in an event certified by AIMS and World Athletics. After the start the course enters corso Sempione and the park, loops around the Castello Sforzesco (km 5), climbs towards Porta Volta (km 7) and Porta Venezia (km 8), then descends into the centre along via Senato and via Manzoni to piazza della Scala (km 10). Then San Babila (km 11) and the inner ring road: piazza Tricolore (km 13), Cinque Giornate, Porta Romana (km 15), Porta Ticinese and the Darsena (km 17), Porta Vercellina (km 18) and back to viale Eginardo. Refreshment points are planned around kilometres 5, 10, 15 and 20, in line with the WA/FIDAL regulation.312

Race services

Official pacers, recognisable by their coloured balloons, will run the half marathon (21 km only, not the 10 km). Anyone following from home can use Trackanza: position, kilometres covered, pace and estimated times by searching an athlete by name or bib number. Rockin'1000 will play live at several points along the course, and an after party at the MiCo, open to everyone, is planned after the finish.23
